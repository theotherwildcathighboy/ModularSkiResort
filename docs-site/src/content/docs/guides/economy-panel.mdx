Resort income: lift tickets, season passes, lessons, lodge spend. The game classes a ski resort as a park, and
parks have no income line in the Economy panel.

<Aside type="caution" title="Unverified Items">
Three items marked (unverified) are not yet confirmed on a live Economy panel.
</Aside>

## Income Booking

Resort income is booked as **public transportation fees** (a chairlift is transport). It reaches the balance
through the game's budget, like any fare.

- **Option:** Options > Modular Ski Resort > Main > Ski area balance > **Book resort income as public transportation fees**. Default on.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Option on (default)">
    **Booking:** tickets, season passes, lessons and lodge spend as public transportation fees, via the game's budget.
    **Economy panel:** **Public transportation** income line; counted in the income total and budget charts. (unverified)
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Option off">
    **Booking:** direct to balance. Treasury result unchanged.
    **Economy panel:** resort never counted, so it shows cost only. Income visible in the hub only.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Closed Income List

The Economy panel has a fixed set of income lines: **4** taxes, **8** service fees, **2** export lines, subsidy.

- **Constraint:** the game builds the list from a fixed set of resource types. A mod cannot add a line without rewriting the panel.
- **Mod policy:** the game's menus are not modified.
- **Closest vanilla label:** *Public transportation* (a lift ticket is a ride fare).
- **Parks:** no fee line exists. Parks only cost.

## Panel Coverage

| Money | In Economy panel | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Lift tickets, season passes, lessons, lodge spend | **Yes**, option on | Income: Public transportation fees |
| Building upkeep and staff wages (sign, lodges, Ski School, patrol) | **Yes** | Expenses: Parks & Recreation |
| Lift running costs (2,500 to 11,000 a month plus a rate per km, by type) | No | Hub, Finances view |
| Run upkeep (per hectare: groomed, snowmaking, night) | No | Hub, Finances view |
| Commercial tax from hotels and shops the resort fills | **Yes**, the largest effect | Income: Commercial tax |

- **Parks & Recreation:** the game's category for park buildings.
- **Hub Finances view:** full total, including lift and run costs. **Net so far** = everything booked this month minus lifts, runs, buildings and wages.

## Commercial Tax

Resort-drawn tourists sleep in hotels and shop in stores. Those companies' profit is taxed on the ordinary
**Commercial tax** line. No mod touches it.

- **Resort income:** no. Excluded from the hub net.
- **Hub row:** **City income the resort brings** = ski area share of the city's tourist draw × game tourist income. Labeled an estimate.
- **Hidden when:** Tourism Overhaul is installed (replaces the game's tourist formula).

## Cross-Check

<Steps>

1. Hub **Finances** view: note **Lift tickets** and the other income rows, month to date.
2. **Economy > Budget**: find the **Public transportation** income line.
3. Both should move together. The panel shows a projected monthly rate that the game updates in steps; expect lag behind the hub, most of all early in a month. (unverified)
4. Treasury change over a month should equal the sum of the panel lines. If not: confirm the option above is on, then see [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting/).

</Steps>

## Other Mods

**Realistic Workplaces and Households** changes staff costs and replaces part of the budget system.

- **Effect:** the game's building and wage bill can differ from the hub figure.
- **Hub notice:** *"Detected: Realistic Workplaces. The real bill can differ."*
- **Fee income crediting:** vanilla behavior expected (unverified).

<Aside type="note" title="Hotel Lodges">
Hotel income comes from a *company* in a rentable building; a park building is neither. A hotel-style income share
implemented in code would be invisible in the panel. The visible form of income sharing with the city is the
commercial tax above, collected from any hotel the resort fills.
</Aside>
