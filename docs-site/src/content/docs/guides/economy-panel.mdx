## Income Booking

Resort income is booked as **Public Transport Fees**, always, and reaches the balance through the game's budget like any fare.

<Shot src="budget.jpg" label="Economy > Budget with a ski resort running: the Public Transport Fees line under Service Fees." />

## Why No Ski Resort Line

The Economy panel has a fixed set of income lines, and this mod does not modify the game's menus. The game classes a ski
resort as a park, and parks have no fee line. *Public Transport Fees* is the closest label: a lift ticket is a ride fare.

## Panel Coverage

| Money | In Economy panel | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Lift tickets, season passes, lessons, lodge spend | **Yes** | Income: Public Transport Fees |
| Building upkeep and staff wages (sign, lodges, Ski School, patrol) | **Yes** | Expenses: Parks & Recreation |
| Lift upkeep (2,500 to 11,000 a month plus a rate per km, by type) | No | Hub, Finances view |
| Run upkeep (per hectare, by feature: grooming, snowmaking, lights, and cat tracks) | No | Hub, Finances view |
| Ski Bus Depot coach bill | No | Hub, Finances view |
| Commercial tax from hotels and shops the resort fills | **Yes**, the largest effect | Income: Commercial tax |

- **Hub Finances view:** full total, including lift and run costs. **Net so far** = everything booked this month minus lifts, runs, ski bus, buildings and wages.

## Commercial Tax

Resort-drawn tourists sleep in hotels and shop in stores, taxed on the ordinary **Commercial tax** line. It is not
resort income and is excluded from the hub net.

- **Hub row:** **City tourist income** = ski area share of the city's tourist draw × game tourist income. Labeled an estimate.
- **Hidden when:** Tourism Overhaul is installed (replaces the game's tourist formula).

## Cross-Check

<Steps>

1. Hub **Finances** view: note **Lift tickets** and the other income rows, month to date.
2. **Economy > Budget**: find the **Public Transport Fees** income line.
3. Both should move together. The panel shows a projected monthly rate that the game updates in steps. Expect lag behind the hub, most of all early in a month. (unverified)
4. Over a month, the treasury changes by the sum of the panel lines minus the lift, run and ski bus costs, which only the hub shows. If it does not, see [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting/).

</Steps>

## Other Mods

**Realistic Workplaces and Households** changes staff costs and replaces part of the budget system.

- **Effect:** the game's building and wage bill can differ from the hub figure.
- **Hub notice:** *"Detected: Realistic Workplaces. The real bill can differ."*
- **Fee income crediting:** vanilla behavior expected (unverified).

<Aside type="note" title="Lodges Are Not Hotels">
Lodges are park buildings and earn no room income. A stay pays the city through the commercial tax of the hotels the resort fills.
</Aside>
