Every price in the mod answers to one of five curves. The graphs and tables here are generated from the mod's code, so they
show the numbers of the version you run. Where the options live: [Passes and Lessons](/guides/passes-and-lessons/). Where the
money lands: [Economy](/guides/economy-panel/).

## Ski-Day Guests and the Lift Ticket

Residents and hotel tourists on a ski day check the lift ticket price before they come. The best lodge in the ski area decides
how much price they accept. The curve multiplies the chance that a guest goes. Snow, distance and attraction come on top.

<DemandChart name="ticket-demand" caption="Ski-day guests who still come, by lift ticket price, for each lodge tier. The dotted line marks the default ticket." />

{/* DEMAND:begin ticket-demand */}
**Equation:** the share of ski-day guests who still come is (240 + tolerance - ticket price) / 200, held between 0 and 1.
The tolerance comes from the best lodge in the ski area. It is **0** for Hut or A-frame, **20** for Chalet Lodge, **40** for Swanky Summit or Grand Lodge.

Up to a ticket of **40** plus the tolerance, everyone comes. Above that, each ticket point costs **0.5 %** of the guests. The price counts up to **200**.

| Ticket price | Hut or A-frame | Chalet | Summit or Grand |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 40 | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % |
| 60 | 90 % | 100 % | 100 % |
| 80 | 80 % | 90 % | 100 % |
| 100 | 70 % | 80 % | 90 % |
| 120 | 60 % | 70 % | 80 % |
| 140 | 50 % | 60 % | 70 % |
| 160 | 40 % | 50 % | 60 % |
| 180 | 30 % | 40 % | 50 % |
| 200 | 20 % | 30 % | 40 % |
{/* DEMAND:end ticket-demand */}

- **Season pass holders:** skip this curve. They already paid.
- **Merged lodges:** count as their tier.
- To keep every ski-day guest, set the ticket at or below the full-demand point of your best lodge tier.

## Season Pass

A resident household buys a season pass when it would ski there, can pay, and the pass looks cheap next to day tickets. The
price is judged against the lift ticket, so the same pass price sells better when the ticket is high.

<DemandChart name="season-pass" caption="Would-be buyers who buy a season pass, by pass price as a multiple of the lift ticket." />

{/* DEMAND:begin season-pass */}
**Equation:** the share of would-be buyers who buy is (12 × ticket - pass price) / (8 × ticket), held between 0 and 1. Everyone buys up to **4 ×** the ticket price. Nobody buys from **12 ×**. The pass price is judged against the ticket price, so raising the ticket moves the whole curve right.

| Pass price | Households that still buy |
| --- | ---: |
| 4 × the ticket | 100 % |
| 6 × the ticket | 75 % |
| 8 × the ticket | 50 % |
| 10 × the ticket | 25 % |
| 12 × the ticket | 0 % |
{/* DEMAND:end season-pass */}

- To sell passes to every would-be buyer, set the pass price at or below the full-demand point in the table.
- The *Season pass share* option sets how many households are would-be buyers in the first place.

## Destination Pass

A tourist party can hold a destination pass for the season. The pass price is the same one as the residents' pass. Holders pay
the daily fee instead of the lift ticket, and they ignore the ticket price at the gate.

<DemandChart name="destination-pass" caption="Tourist parties on a destination pass, by pass price, for the lowest and highest lodge tier." />

{/* DEMAND:begin destination-pass */}
**Equations:** the daily fee a pass holder pays is the pass price / 4, rounded, never above the lift ticket and never below 1. The share of tourist parties on a pass is *Season pass share* × the season pass demand above × the ski-day curve above. The curve is applied to the **fee**, not to the ticket. The share cannot pass 100 %.

The table is for a lift ticket of **100** and the default *Season pass share* of **50 %**.

| Pass price | Daily fee | Parties on a pass, Hut or A-frame | Parties on a pass, Summit or Grand |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 200 | 50 | 48 % | 50 % |
| 400 | 100 | 35 % | 45 % |
| 600 | 100 | 26 % | 34 % |
| 800 | 100 | 18 % | 23 % |
| 1000 | 100 | 9 % | 11 % |
| 1200 | 100 | 0 % | 0 % |
{/* DEMAND:end destination-pass */}

- The fee follows the pass price, so a cheap pass means a cheap day for the holder. The fee never goes above the ticket.
- To get more parties on passes, lower the pass price or raise the lodge tier.

## Game-Sent Tourist Parties

The game sends some tourist parties to the ski area on its own, as attraction outings. They pay the lift ticket at the gate.
Their curve is gentler than the one for ski-day guests, because the ticket is a small part of a trip with travel and a hotel.

<DemandChart name="tourist-party" caption="Share of game-sent tourist parties that pay at the gate, against the ski-day curve." />

{/* DEMAND:begin tourist-party */}
**Equation:** the share of game-sent tourist parties that pay at the gate is (300 + tolerance - ticket price) / 200, held between 0 and 1. It is the ski-day curve moved **60** to the right, with the same slope.

Up to a ticket of **100** plus the tolerance, every party pays (the default ticket is **100**). At **200** it is **50 %**, or **70 %** with a Swanky Summit or Grand Lodge. A party that does not pay walks away: no ticket, no lesson, no lodge spend. Holders of a destination pass never walk away.

| Ticket price | Ski-day guests, Hut or A-frame | Tourist parties, Hut or A-frame | Tourist parties, Chalet | Tourist parties, Summit or Grand |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 | 70 % | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % |
| 120 | 60 % | 90 % | 100 % | 100 % |
| 140 | 50 % | 80 % | 90 % | 100 % |
| 160 | 40 % | 70 % | 80 % | 90 % |
| 180 | 30 % | 60 % | 70 % | 80 % |
| 200 | 20 % | 50 % | 60 % | 70 % |
{/* DEMAND:end tourist-party */}

- At the default ticket, nothing changes for these parties.
- To keep every game-sent party, keep the ticket at or below the default, plus the lodge tier's tolerance.

## Lodge Spend

Each paying ski guest spends at the arrival lodge. The *Lodge spend* option is the spend at the default ticket. A higher ticket
raises it, but slower than the ticket itself.

<DemandChart name="lodge-spend" caption="Lodge spend per paying guest, by lift ticket price, for each lodge tier." />

{/* DEMAND:begin lodge-spend */}
**Equation:** spend per paying guest = *Lodge spend* setting × tier multiplier × square root of (ticket price / 100), rounded to a whole number. The multiplier is **1** for Hut or A-frame, **1.5** for Chalet Lodge, **3** for Swanky Summit or Grand Lodge. At a ticket of **100** the root is 1, so the table reads as the setting alone.

The table is for the default setting of **15**. With tickets off there is no gate and no spend.

| Ticket price | Hut or A-frame | Chalet Lodge | Swanky Summit or Grand Lodge |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 25 | 8 | 11 | 23 |
| 50 | 11 | 16 | 32 |
| 80 | 13 | 20 | 40 |
| 100 | 15 | 23 | 45 |
| 150 | 18 | 28 | 55 |
| 200 | 21 | 32 | 64 |
{/* DEMAND:end lodge-spend */}

- To lift spend per guest without touching the ticket, build a higher lodge tier.
- A guest who cannot pay the ticket and the spend together pays the ticket first. The spend is limited to what is left in the household.
