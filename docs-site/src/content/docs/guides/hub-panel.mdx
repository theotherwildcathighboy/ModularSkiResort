The **Ski Resort hub** is the mod's single panel.

- **Open:** **Ski Resort** button (top left), Universal Mod Panel, or selecting a Welcome Sign (option *Open the hub when a Welcome Sign is selected*).
- **Close:** X.

<Shot src="hub.jpg" label="The whole hub in winter on a built resort: header, status line, tiles, one open view, build strip." />

## Frame

Persistent elements above the views.

- **Header:** one ski area, keyed to its Welcome Sign. Arrows: switch ski area. Fly button: jump to the sign. Pen: rename (empty name restores the default). Runs and lifts outside every sign's radius (**5 km** by default, option **Ski area radius**) are listed as *Not linked to a ski area* and score **0**.
- **Status line:** *Open*, *Closed* or *Patchy*, with *· 1 patchy* while some runs are patchy and *≈* while the snow is estimated. On its right: snow depth and cover (*124 cm · 100 %*), *Powder day*, or a season warning. Tooltip: open, patchy and closed run counts, snow rule (open at **91 cm** on **60 %** of a run; an open run closes when **30 cm** covers under **40 %**), measured or estimated snow. The hub shows depths in your game's unit setting.
- **Problem band:** one fact per line (*No lift yet*, *Lodge or school: road required*, *Lift X: no power*). Hover: cost. Click: fly to the cause or open the list. See [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting/).
- **Tiles:** Attraction, Runs, Lifts, Guests, Finances, History. Click opens the view; tooltip lists figures.
- **Start card:** while the city has no Welcome Sign. Replaces all content with the four steps to a first resort.

## Attraction

Ski area score on the game's attraction scale: large park about **10**, tourist attraction **50**, landmark **120**, cap **200**.

- **Receipt:** one row per term. **Lodge base**, then *Runs, lifts and school*: **Runs**, **Lifts**, **Ski school**, **Variety**, **Vertical** and **Scale** (scale × the square root of the terms above), then **Efficiency** (as in the Welcome Sign's info panel) and the result, **Attraction**.
- **Section header tooltip:** the full equation. Derivation: [Attraction Formula](/reference/attraction/).
- **Zero rows:** a row at 0 with a known cause shows a warning glyph (*No ski school*, *No green run*).
- **Mountain section:** run difficulty mix, **vertical** (lowest run bottom to highest run top), **skiable area**, **snow depth**, **run coverage**. **≈** = estimate.
- **Share of the city's tourist draw:** this ski area's slice of the city's attraction, game method (each building's score squared). Sets how many tourists arrive; the destination is unaffected.

## Runs

Per run: difficulty, lift service (**↑** column), length, drop, area, state.

- **State:** Open, Patchy or Closed, with snow percentage.
- **Row click:** fly to the run. **Pen:** rename.
- **Also here:** tree tool (plans trees on and around runs), **piste map** switch.
- **Resort Planner open:** its rows replace the tree tool's at the top: Scan, Build, Undo (Stop while building), the scan progress or the plan's totals, the Mix, How many, Features, Lifts and Way home knobs, one row per lift and per pod with no lift (click one to leave it out or take it back), then the tree filler. See [Runs and Snow](/guides/runs-and-snow/#resort-planner-experimental).

## Lifts

Per lift: type, length, rise, ride time, capacity (skiers per hour), served runs, running cost.

- **Problem states:** *unfinished*, *section missing*, *no power*.
- **Total row:** length and capacity summed across working lifts.
- **Capacity:** design figure. Skier models are not shown on screen.

## Guests

Counts tourists arriving at the ski area's lodge or Welcome Sign, and residents arriving at a lodge for leisure.

- **Rows:** ski-day trips, trips that reached a lodge, tourists, tourist groups, residents, lift tickets paid, season-pass arrivals, lessons, ticket money.
- **Car spaces:** one row per active lodge with a road: its car spaces within **300 m**, counted per lodge. Two or more lodges of one tier in a ski area are numbered (*Chalet 1*, *Chalet 2*); a lodge keeps its number while it exists and stays in that ski area. A lodge renamed in its info panel shows that name instead, at once. Click: fly to the lodge.
- **Bars:** **this month** against **last month** ("March").
- **State line:** ski days off, closed, or *Full right now*, with the limiting factor (runs or lodges).
- **Tourism mod replacing the game formula** (such as Tourism Overhaul): notice shown, no tourist estimate.

## Finances

Income above, running costs below, **Net so far** at the bottom. Rows sum to the net.

- **Income:** lift tickets, lodge spend, season passes sold, lessons.
- **Running costs:** lifts, runs, building upkeep, staff wages, and *Ski bus* while the ski area has a [Ski Bus Depot](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/#ski-bus-depot).
- **Excluded from net:** pass holders skiing free; *City tourist income* (estimate of extra tourist spend elsewhere in the city).
- **Buildings and wages:** today's rate, prorated to the share of the month elapsed.
- **Lift ticket price line:** shows the ski-day response to price.
- **Economy panel mapping:** [Economy](/guides/economy-panel/).

## History

<Icon name="glyph-history" alt="History tile glyph" /> Guests, finances and attraction per month.

- **Scope:** *This ski area* or *Whole city* (every ski area that exists now; a deleted one's history goes with it).
- **Ranges:** *12 months*; *This season vs last* (last season's same months in gray behind); *By season* (one bar per season, the average month, so incomplete seasons compare fairly).
- **Shown:** *Guests*, *Finances*, *Both*, or *Attraction* (the score and the snow when each month closed; one ski area only).
- **Bar tooltip:** numbers.
- **Faded bars:** partial months. **Hatched slots:** nothing recorded.
- **First month:** closes at the end of the month the ski area starts in.

## Build Strip

Three tabs along the bottom hold every tool.

- **Lifts:** base station, top station, and the lift line tool (**Ctrl+Shift+L**). Tile icons: <Icon name="lift-fixed-grip" alt="Fixed-grip lift icon" /> <Icon name="lift-detachable" alt="Detachable lift icon" /> <Icon name="lift-gondola" alt="Gondola icon" /> <Icon name="lift-tram" alt="Aerial tram icon" />
- **Runs:** Run and Cat track. While the Run tile is active, a row sets the next run's features: Groomed, Snow guns and Lights (see [Run Features](/guides/runs-and-snow/#run-features)); its tooltip lists each feature's price. The cat track tile opens the cat track tool, **Ctrl+Shift+K** (see [Runs and Snow](/guides/runs-and-snow/#cat-track-tool)). Beside the Tree tool sits **Resort Planner (experimental)**: outline a slope, get proposed lifts and a set of runs, build the runs. It is off until you turn it on in Options ([Runs and Snow](/guides/runs-and-snow/#resort-planner-experimental)). The **Fence** tile places the Safety Net Fence, decorative orange netting. Tile icons: <Icon name="run-groomed" alt="Run icon" /> <Icon name="run-cat-track" alt="Cat track icon" />
- **Buildings:** lodges, Ski School, patrol.
- **Tiles:** cost shown; the active tile reads *In use*. Click it again to stop.

<Aside type="note" title="Standalone Buildings">
Lodges, Ski School and patrol are ordinary buildings, placed with the game's building tool like any other. A tile
only starts the placement. A building within the ski area radius (**5 km** by default) of a Welcome Sign belongs to the nearest one.
</Aside>
