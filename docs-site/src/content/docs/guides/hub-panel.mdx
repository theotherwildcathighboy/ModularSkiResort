The **Ski Resort hub** is the mod's single panel.

**Open:** **Ski Resort** button (top left), Universal Mod Panel, or selecting a Welcome Sign (option *Open the hub when a Welcome Sign is selected*).

<Shot src="hub.jpg" label="The whole hub on a built resort: header, status line, problem line, tiles, the Attraction view and the open build strip." />

## Frame

- **Header:** one ski area, keyed to its Welcome Sign. The name opens the list of ski areas. Pen: rename (an empty name restores the default). Info button: [Symbols](#hub-symbols). Runs and lifts outside every sign's radius (**5 km** by default, option **Ski area radius**) are listed as *Not linked to a ski area* and score **0**.
- **Status line:** *Open* (every run is open), *Partly open* or *Closed*, with snow depth and cover, *Powder day* or a season warning. *≈* marks estimated snow. Tooltip: run counts by state, and whether the snow is measured or estimated. The rule: [Snow Conditions](/guides/runs-and-snow/#snow-conditions).
- **Problem line:** the worst problem (*Lodge or school: road required*), with a button for the rest. Hover: why it matters, and for a missing lodge, stopped lodges or low efficiency, the attraction score once it is fixed. Click: fly to the cause. See [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting/).
- **Tiles:** Attraction, Runs open, Lifts, Guests, Finances, History. Each opens its view.
- **Start card:** while the city has no Welcome Sign. Replaces all content with the four steps to a first resort.

## Attraction

Ski area score on the game's attraction scale, **0** to **200** (a landmark is **120**). Derivation: [Attraction Formula](/reference/attraction/).

- **Receipt:** **Lodge base**, the fold *Runs, lifts and school* (**Runs**, **Lifts**, **Ski school**, **Variety**, **Vertical**), **Efficiency** and the result, **Attraction**. The fold's number is the attraction scale × the square root of its terms combined. The section header tooltip has the full equation.
- **Missing lines:** a row tooltip ends with what the row misses, in yellow (*Missing: blue run*, *Not in full: 2 closed · 1 no lift*).
- **Mountain fold:** the **vertical** (lowest run bottom to highest run top), the run difficulty mix and the **skiable area**.
- **Merged buildings:** a fold, while the ski area has one, with the buildings merged in as lodges and their tier or state. See [Merged Buildings](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/#merged-buildings).
- **Share of city tourism:** sets how many tourists come to the city, not where they go. See [Tourist Draw](/reference/attraction/#tourist-draw).

## Runs

Per run: a state dot, the difficulty, the name, lift service (**↑** column), length and drop.

- **Row click:** fly to the run and show its feature rows (Groomed, Snow guns, Lights) above the list. A cat track's trash button is there too.
- **Also here:** the **Piste map** switch, the tree rows while the Tree tool is on, and the Resort Planner's rows and lift list ([Resort Planner](/guides/runs-and-snow/#resort-planner-experimental)).

## Lifts

Per lift: a status mark, the name and a status word, then capacity (skiers per hour) and the number of runs it serves. The lift type shows while the ski area mixes types. Tooltip: length, rise, ride time and upkeep.

- **Status words:** *Running*, *No power*, *Chairs stopped*, *Section missing*, *Unfinished*, *Closed*.
- **Total row:** *Capacity, all lifts*, summed across complete lifts with power and an open run. Its tooltip has the total length.

## Guests

Counts tourists arriving at the ski area's lodge or Welcome Sign, and residents arriving at a lodge for leisure.

- **Folds:** *At the lodges* (**Tourists**, **Residents**. The Tourists tooltip counts the tourist groups), *Ski-day trips* (**Reached a lodge**, with the state line) and *Lift tickets* (**Paid**, **Season pass**, **Lessons**). The ticket money is in Finances.
- **Tourists drawn ≈:** an estimate from the game's tourist formula.
- **Car spaces:** one row per active lodge with a road: its car spaces within **600 m**. Lodges of one building type are numbered (*Chalet 1*, *Chalet 2*) unless renamed.
- **State line:** ski days off, closed, or *Full right now*, with the limiting factor (runs or lodges).
- **Tourism mod replacing the game formula** (such as Tourism Overhaul): notice shown, no tourist estimate.

## Finances

Three rows, as in the game's budget panel: **Income** (a fold), **Running costs** (a fold) and **Net so far**. The rows in the two folds sum to the net.

- **Income:** lift tickets, lodge spend, season passes sold, lessons.
- **Running costs:** lifts, runs, building upkeep, staff wages, and *Ski bus* while the ski area has a [Ski Bus Depot](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/#ski-bus-depot).
- **Excluded from net:** pass holders skiing free, and *City tourist income* (estimate of extra tourist spend elsewhere in the city).
- **Buildings and wages:** today's rate, prorated to the share of the month elapsed.
- **Economy panel mapping:** [Economy](/guides/economy-panel/).

## History

<Icon name="glyph-history" alt="History tile glyph" /> Guests, finances and attraction per month.

- **Scope:** *This ski area* or *Whole city* (every ski area that exists now). The history of a deleted ski area goes with it.
- **Ranges:** *12 months*, *This season vs last* (last season's same months in gray behind), or *By season* (one bar per season, the average month, so incomplete seasons compare fairly).
- **Charts:** guests and finances, always both. With *This ski area*, an Attraction strip under them shows the score, and on hover the snow, when each month closed.
- **Faded bars:** partial months. **Hatched slots:** nothing recorded.
- **First month:** closes at the end of the month the ski area starts in.

## Build Strip

Three tabs along the bottom hold every tool: a row of chips, a row of tiles and a status line. The strip stays open while a tool it started is in use.

- **Lifts:** chips for the lift type (Fixed-grip, Detachable, Gondola, Tram) and the **Lift tool** (**Ctrl+Shift+L**). Tiles: Base station, Top station, Lift line. Tile icons: <Icon name="lift-fixed-grip" alt="Fixed-grip lift icon" /> <Icon name="lift-detachable" alt="Detachable lift icon" /> <Icon name="lift-gondola" alt="Gondola icon" /> <Icon name="lift-tram" alt="Aerial tram icon" />
- **Runs:** tiles Run ([Run Features](/guides/runs-and-snow/#run-features)), Cat track (**Ctrl+Shift+K**, [Cat Track Tool](/guides/runs-and-snow/#cat-track-tool)) and Fence (the Safety Net Fence, decorative orange netting). Chips: **Tree tool**, **Resort Planner** (off until turned on in Options) and **Delete cat tracks**. Tile icons: <Icon name="run-groomed" alt="Run icon" /> <Icon name="run-cat-track" alt="Cat track icon" />
- **Buildings:** chip **Lodges**: the Welcome Sign (*Sign*) and the five lodges. Chip **Services**: the Ski Bus Depot, the Ski School and the two patrol buildings. Chip **Mergeable buildings**: see [Merged Buildings](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/#merged-buildings).
- **Padlock:** the tile is not available yet. Click it: the status line gives the reason.
- **Yellow dot:** one at a time. It marks the tile that solves the first problem on the problem line (with no Welcome Sign: the Sign tile). When that tile is on another tab or behind another chip, the dot sits on the tab or the chip.
