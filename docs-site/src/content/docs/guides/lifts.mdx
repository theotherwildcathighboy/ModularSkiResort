A lift is a **base station**, a **top station** and the **lift line** between them. One lift tool draws the line for
all four types; it plans the towers, and the cable and carriers are generated.

<Aside type="note" title="Lifts Make Runs Work">
A run reaches full value only while a lift serves it; a run no lift serves counts as backcountry (**40 %** by default).
Which runs a lift serves, and the distances: [Runs and Snow, Lift Service](/guides/runs-and-snow/#lift-service).
</Aside>

## Build Order

<Steps>

1. **Lift type:** hub Lifts tab. Line and both stations share one type.
   <Icon name="lift-fixed-grip" alt="Fixed-grip lift icon" /> Fixed-grip triple chairlift.
   <Icon name="lift-detachable" alt="Detachable lift icon" /> Detachable quad chairlift.
   <Icon name="lift-gondola" alt="Gondola icon" /> Gondola.
   <Icon name="lift-tram" alt="Aerial tram icon" /> Aerial tram.
2. **Base station:** bottom of the lift.
3. **Top station:** top of the lift, facing the base station. A guide line shows when the stations are straight to each other.
4. **Lift line:** open the lift tool from the hub (the line tile of the type) or with **Ctrl+Shift+L**. Click the base station, then the top station. The tool plans the towers and shows them before anything is built.
5. **Adjust, then build:** press **Enter**. See Lift Tool below.
6. **Power:** low-voltage line to the base station.

</Steps>

## Lift Tool

One input model for every lift type. The prompt at the top of the screen names what the next click does.

- **Start:** click a station, a loose line end or open ground; then click the other end.
- **Add a tower:** click the line. A tower you add is pinned.
- **Move a tower:** drag it. It is pinned where you drop it (snapped to a 5 m step, tram 10 m). Every pinned tower stays exactly where it is; the planner refits only the unpinned towers between them, and still sets every tower's height. A pin closer than 10 m to a station, or closer than 10 m (tram 40 m) to another pin, is refused. Pinned towers show a blue inner ring.
- **Remove a tower:** drag it off the line and release. A pinned tower becomes unpinned; the planner puts a tower back near it only if the line needs one there.
- **Step back:** right click undoes one action. With nothing left to undo it closes the tool.
- **Build:** **Enter**. **Esc** closes the tool without building.
- **Preview:** the cable is colored by how far its lowest carrier clears the ground beyond the required minimum (**3 m**, tram **5 m**), so a bad line shows before it is built:
  - **Green:** at least **2 m** to spare.
  - **Amber:** 0 to 2 m to spare. Clear, but tight.
  - **Red:** below the minimum. The carriers would be too low; the tower ring is red too (*short*).
  - **Violet:** the cable is more than **30 m** above the ground (chairlifts and gondolas; the tram has no limit); the tower ring is violet.
  - **Tooltip:** span lengths and the lowest carrier height; it turns to a warning while any span is red or violet. Add or move a tower to fix it. The tool does not block building a flagged line.
- **Planner:** fits tower heights to the terrain with few towers. Next to a station it prefers the shortest tower, and may add one tower to get shorter ones there. It plans within the steepest span the line allows. Option *Plan lift towers*, default on.
- **Too steep:** a span steeper than the line type allows is drawn in red and the tower ring turns red. Add or move a tower, or change the route.
- **Tower numbers:** every tower shows its number, counted up the line from the base station, 1 to 200, then back to 1. All chairs show the same number.
- **Already-lined station:** refused. Bulldoze a section first, then click its two loose ends.

## Section Redo

The line consists of sections. Each section is an independent edit.

- **Steep or uneven ground:** draw one section at a time.
- **Wrong section:** bulldoze or redraw that section. Remaining sections keep towers, cable and carriers.
- **Missing section:** problem *"Lift X is missing a section"*. The lift counts **0** and is billed **a quarter** of its running cost until the line is complete. Click the problem to fly to the gap.

<Aside type="tip" title="Bulldozer Warning">
Option **Lift building aids** (default on): the bulldozer warns what a click on a lift removes.
</Aside>

## Terrain

Limits per lift type:

- **Steepest slope:** fixed-grip **80 %**, detachable **90 %**, gondola **120 %**, aerial tram **250 %**.
- **Longest tower span:** fixed-grip **90 m**, detachable **245 m**, gondola **245 m**, tram **3,000 m** (the length of the longest real free spans, such as Whistler's Peak 2 Peak). The planner adds towers to stay under it, and also wherever the sagging rope would come too close to the ground.
- **Towers:** height follows terrain; concrete foundations; grounding matches vanilla power pylons on sensible lines. Heights come in steps: see [Lift Types](/reference/lifts/).
- **Canyons and cliffs:** the planner puts towers where the ground rises toward the cable and uses tall towers where they let a span reach farther, so crossings take fewer towers. Carriers stay at least **3 m** above the ground (tram **5 m**); chairlift and gondola cable stays at most **30 m** above it. If no mix of tower heights clears a span, the preview shows red or violet (see Preview above): add or move a tower, or route the line elsewhere.
- **Stations:** sit on the slope as placed; no terrain flattening. The one exception is the aerial tram's base terminal, which levels a flat pad by design. On steep ground a station can end up partly in the hillside, so the chairs leave it below the ground; the tool then shows a notice and still builds. Raise the station with Move It, or build on flatter ground. Adjust the ground with the game's terraforming, or reposition a station with Move It.

## Power

A lift line is a power-line network, so it behaves like one.

- **Electricity overlay:** the lift line and the stations' line-side stubs are high-voltage class (that is what lets the line snap to the stations), so they draw as high-voltage lines. Only the base station's own stub is low-voltage.
- **Base station = transformer:** wire a low-voltage line into the base station's low-voltage stub. Only the base station needs power: the top station has no consumer, and a lift counts as powered from its base. The base joins its stub to the lift line like a vanilla transformer, but the line ends at the top station, so a lift carries no city power anywhere and does not add grid capacity.
- **City high-voltage lines:** do not connect them to a lift tower or lift line. The result is untested and can break the lift.

## Uninstall

Bulldoze all lifts before removing the mod. Leftover lift pieces turn into missing-prefab placeholders.

## Known Limits

- **Riders:** chairs and cabins move along the cable empty. Skier character models are not shown on screen; the guests are real in the economy.
- **Wind:** the cable moves in the wind; the chairs do not sway with it (engine limit).

## Scoring

Conditions for a lift to add to the ski area score:

- **Complete:** two stations and a joined line.
- **Powered:** base station has power. An unpowered lift stops, adds **0** and still pays maintenance.

Values per type: [Attraction Formula](/reference/attraction/). Speeds, seats and prices: [Lift Types](/reference/lifts/).

**Run service:** a run counts at full value while a running lift serves it. Distances and rules: [Runs and Snow, Lift Service](/guides/runs-and-snow/#lift-service).

## Problem States

| Hub line | Condition |
|---|---|
| *Lift X has no power* | Base station without electricity. Connect a low-voltage line. |
| *Lift X is missing a section* | Section removed or being redrawn. |
| *Lift X is unfinished* | Line does not join a base station and a top station. |
| *Lift X: draw the lift line* | Both stations placed, no line between them. |
| *Lift X mixes lift types* | Line and stations differ in type. Redraw the line as the station type. |
| *Lift X: chairs stopped* | Lift complete, still counts; carriers stopped. Cause in the mod log: [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting/). |

## Recoloring

- **Stations:** the game's color picker (steel and safety padding).
- **Line towers:** the game's color tool works by selecting an object, and towers that belong to a lift line cannot be selected. The optional Recolor mod's painter tool can paint them.
