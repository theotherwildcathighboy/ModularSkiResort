A lift is a **base station**, a **top station** and the **lift line** between them. One lift tool draws the line for
all four types. It plans the towers. The rope and carriers are generated.

<Aside type="note" title="Lifts Make Runs Work">
A run reaches full value only while a lift serves it. A run no lift serves counts as backcountry (**40 %** by default).
Which runs a lift serves, and the distances: [Runs and Snow, Lift Service](/guides/runs-and-snow/#lift-service).
</Aside>

## Build Order

<Steps>

1. **Lift type:** hub Lifts tab. Line and both stations share one type.
   <Icon name="lift-fixed-grip" alt="Fixed-grip lift icon" /> Fixed-grip triple chairlift.
   <Icon name="lift-detachable" alt="Detachable lift icon" /> Detachable quad chairlift.
   <Icon name="lift-gondola" alt="Gondola icon" /> Gondola.
   <Icon name="lift-tram" alt="Aerial tram icon" /> Aerial tram.
2. **Base station:** bottom of the lift.
3. **Top station:** top of the lift, facing the base station. A guide line shows when the stations are straight to each other.
4. **Lift line:** open the lift tool from the hub (the line tile of the type) or with **Ctrl+Shift+L**. Click the base station, then the top station. The tool plans the towers and shows them before anything is built.
5. **Adjust, then build:** press **Enter**. See Lift Tool below.
6. **Power:** low-voltage line to the base station.

</Steps>

## Lift Tool

One input model for every lift type. The prompt at the top of the screen names what the next click does.

- **Start:** click a station, a loose line end or open ground. Then click the other end.
- **Add a tower:** click the line. A tower you add is pinned.
- **Move a tower:** drag it. It is pinned where you drop it (snapped to a 5 m step, tram 10 m). Every pinned tower stays exactly where it is. The planner refits only the unpinned towers between them and still sets every tower's height. A pin closer than 10 m to a station, or closer than 10 m (tram 40 m) to another pin, is refused. Pinned towers show a blue inner ring.
- **Remove a tower:** drag it off the line and release. A pinned tower becomes unpinned. The planner puts a tower back near it only if the line needs one there.
- **Step back:** right click undoes one action. With nothing left to undo it closes the tool.
- **Build:** **Enter**. **Esc** closes the tool without building.
- **Preview:** the rope is colored by how far its lowest carrier clears the ground beyond the required minimum (**3 m**, tram **5 m**). A bad line shows before it is built:
  - **Green:** at least **2 m** to spare.
  - **Amber:** 0 to 2 m to spare. Clear, but tight.
  - **Red:** below the minimum. The carriers would be too low. The tower ring is red too (*short*).
  - **Violet:** the rope is more than **30 m** above the ground. This applies to chairlifts and gondolas. The tram has no limit. The tower ring is violet.
  - **Tooltip:** span lengths and the lowest carrier height. It turns to a warning while any span is red or violet. Add or move a tower to fix it. The tool does not block building a flagged line.
- **Planner:** fits tower heights to the terrain with few towers. Next to a station it prefers the shortest tower, and may add one tower to get shorter ones there. It plans within the steepest span the line allows. Option *Plan lift towers*, default on.
- **Too steep:** a span steeper than the line type allows is drawn in red and the tower ring turns red. Add or move a tower, or change the route.
- **Red tower:** the planner first moves towers, picks other heights, adds a pair or drops a tower. A tower it cannot fix turns red, and its tooltip names the reason. The line still builds and runs:
  - *Too steep for this lift type:* the rope runs through one tower steeper than fixed-grip **38°**, detachable **40°**, gondola **40°**, aerial tram **55°**.
  - *Past what its wheels can follow:* the rope bends at the tower more than its sheave train follows.
  - *Past what tram saddles are built for:* the rope bends over a tram tower's saddle by more than **40°** (**20°** each side).
  - *Past where chairs or cabins clear the tower:* the rope leaves the tower so steeply that a hanging chair or cabin would swing into the tower head.
- **Trees:** a built line fells the trees in its corridor: **11.4 m** wide on a fixed-grip, **13.6 m** detachable, **14.2 m** gondola, **20.8 m** tram, plus any tree whose crown reaches the carriers. The preview shows the cut. A refused build keeps every tree.
- **Tower numbers:** every tower shows its number, counted up the line from the base station, 1 to 200, then back to 1. All chairs show the same number.
- **Station that already has a line:** refused. Bulldoze a section first, then click its two loose ends.

## Section Redo

The line consists of sections. Each section is an independent edit.

- **Steep or uneven ground:** draw one section at a time.
- **Wrong section:** bulldoze or redraw that section. Remaining sections keep towers, rope and carriers.
- **Missing section:** problem *"Lift X: section missing"*. The lift counts **0** and pays **a quarter** of its upkeep until the line is complete. Click the problem to fly to the gap.

<Aside type="tip" title="Bulldozer Warning">
Option **Lift building aids** (default on): the bulldozer warns what a click on a lift removes.
</Aside>

## Terrain

Limits per lift type:

- **Steepest slope:** fixed-grip **80 %**, detachable **90 %**, gondola **120 %**, aerial tram **250 %**.
- **Longest tower span:** fixed-grip **90 m**, detachable **245 m**, gondola **245 m**, tram **3,000 m** (about the longest free span between two real towers: Whistler's Peak 2 Peak gondola spans 3.0 km unsupported on its 4.4 km line). The planner adds towers to stay under it, and also wherever the sagging rope would come too close to the ground.
- **Towers:** height follows terrain. Foundations are concrete. Grounding matches vanilla power pylons on sensible lines. Heights come in steps: see [Lift Types](/reference/lifts/).
- **Steep lines:** where the rope runs steeply through a tower, the whole tower leans downhill from its foot, as on real steep lifts: from **12°** of rope slope on a fixed-grip, **20°** on a detachable or gondola, **30°** on a tram, by at most **25°** (detachable and gondola **20°**). The head and wheels tilt with the rope on top of that. Every tower's padded side faces uphill, whichever way the line was drawn. Tram towers stand on concrete pedestals. Where the ground does not fall away with the lean, a leaning tram tower stands higher on them so its steel stays clear of the ground.
- **Canyons and cliffs:** the planner puts towers where the ground rises toward the rope. It uses tall towers where they let a span reach farther, so crossings take fewer towers. Carriers stay at least **3 m** above the ground (tram **5 m**). Chairlift and gondola rope stays at most **30 m** above it. If no mix of tower heights clears a span, the preview shows red or violet (see Preview above). Add or move a tower, or route the line elsewhere.
- **Stations:** the chairlift and gondola stations cut a level pad into the slope where you place them. They cut the ground above the pad and fill nothing, so on the downhill side the slope stays below the station. A base station cuts its pad 12 m past its lot on the line side, so the chairs leave it over level ground. The aerial tram's base terminal levels its pad (cut and fill). The aerial tram's top terminal keeps the slope as placed. Where the ground past the pad rises faster than the chairs climb, the chairs leave below the ground. The tool then shows a notice and still builds. Build on flatter ground, adjust the ground with the game's terraforming, or reposition a station with Move It.
- **Crossing another lift:** another lift is passed over, or under when the other lift runs high there. The towers beside the crossing are made tall enough for the clearance the game requires. Where that is not possible, the line is not built. The notice says why: no tower arrangement reaches the clearance, the crossing is too close to a station or to one of the other lift's towers, or the two meet at an angle under **20°**. Two lifts of the same type cannot cross: the game cannot keep them apart there, so that line is not built either.
- **Roads:** the planner keeps towers off roads, rail and tram tracks, and paths under the line. Roads and paths that belong to a building (a parking lot, a park's paths) are not kept free. Where the line has no clear plan without a tower on a road, the tower stands there. The game reports it as overlapping: move the road or the tower.
- **Cat tracks:** build them before the lifts that pass over them. The cat track tool refuses to grade within **6 m** of a lift line, so a track can never go under a lift that is already built.
- **Along another lift:** a line that runs on top of another lift (within **8 m** and **10°** of it, over more than **15 m**) is not built. The game would merge the two lines.

## Power

A lift line is a power-line network, so it behaves like one.

- **Icons:** a station with no lift line, and a loose line end, show the yellow **Lift line needed** icon. It clears when a line is attached there. The base station also shows the game's low-voltage "not connected" icon until its feed is wired. A lift shows no other power icon: a lift without power because the city is short of it shows as such in the hub's lift status.
- **Electricity overlay:** placing a lift line or a station does not open it. Opened by hand, it draws the lift line and the stations' line stubs as high-voltage lines (that is what lets the line snap to the stations). Its hover tooltip shows their power figures (engine limit). Only the base station's own stub is low-voltage.
- **Info panel and asset menu:** a station's info panel has the game's Transformer section. The asset menu lists transformer and power line capacities for stations and lift lines (engine limit: the game reads them from the parts that power the lift).
- **Experience:** drawing a lift line earns the game's experience for power lines, and the experience note says so (engine limit).
- **Base station = transformer:** wire a low-voltage line into the base station's low-voltage stub. Only the base station needs power. The top station has no consumer, and a lift counts as powered from its base. The base joins its stub to the lift line like a vanilla transformer. But the line ends at the top station, so a lift carries no city power anywhere and does not add grid capacity.
- **City high-voltage lines:** they cannot join a lift tower, stub or lift line. The game refuses them with a red preview.

## Uninstall

Before removing the mod, clear the city of it, then save:

- **Delete all:** Options > Modular Ski Resort > Reset > **Delete all ski resort content** removes every lift, run and building of the mod ([Settings, Reset](/reference/settings/#reset)).
- **By hand:** bulldoze all lifts and all runs.

What stays if you skip this, measured with the mod's files moved away from a city of 337,000 (the city loaded and ran without errors):

- **Buildings and stations:** checkered placeholder blocks at full size.
- **Lift lines:** bare strips, with no chairs or towers.
- **Runs:** flat cream patches with no snow.

## Known Limits

- **Riders:** chairs and cabins move along the rope empty. Skier character models are not shown on screen. The guests are real in the economy.
- **Chair numbers:** towers are numbered. Chairs all show the same number.
- **Trees:** the line fells the trees along its whole corridor, also where the rope runs high above them.
- **More:** the full list is on the [front page](/#known-limitations).

## Scoring

Conditions for a lift to add to the ski area score:

- **Complete:** two stations and a joined line.
- **Powered:** base station has power. An unpowered lift stops, adds **0** and still pays upkeep.

Values per type: [Attraction Formula](/reference/attraction/). Speeds, seats and prices: [Lift Types](/reference/lifts/).

**Run service:** a run counts at full value while a running lift serves it. Distances and rules: [Runs and Snow, Lift Service](/guides/runs-and-snow/#lift-service).

## Problem States

| Hub line | Condition |
|---|---|
| *Lift X: no power* | Base station without power. Connect a low-voltage line. |
| *Lift X: section missing* | Section removed or being redrawn. |
| *Lift X: unfinished* | Line does not join a base station and a top station. |
| *Lift X: no lift line yet* | Both stations placed, no line between them. |
| *Lift X: no top station yet* / *no base station yet* | One station placed. |
| *Lift X mixes lift types* | Line and stations differ in type. Redraw the line as the station type. |
| *Lift X: tower N's wheels cannot follow the rope* | A tower's rope bends or pitches past what its wheels follow, relative to the tower's lean. Warning only. The lift runs and counts. |
| *Lift X: rope turns N° at tower N* | The line bends at a tower by more than **5°**, more than real lines do. Warning only. The lift runs and counts. |
| *Lift X: chairs pass N m above the ground* (or *touch the ground*) | A span's carriers come closer to the ground than planned lines allow (**3 m**, tram **5 m**). Warning only. The lift tool replans a section you redraw. |
| *Lift X: chairs stopped* | Lift complete, still counts. Carriers stopped. Cause in the mod log: [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting/). |

## Recoloring

- **Stations:** the game's color picker (steel and safety padding).
- **Line towers:** the game's color tool works by selecting an object, and towers that belong to a lift line cannot be selected. The optional Recolor mod's painter tool can paint them.
