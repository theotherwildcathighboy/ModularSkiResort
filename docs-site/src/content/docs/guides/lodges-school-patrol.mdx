## Lodges

<Shot src="lodge-day.jpg" label="An A-Frame Day Lodge at a road." />

Guest entry point and skier room source. No lodge: attraction score **0**.

| Building | Tier | Role |
|---|---|---|
| <Icon name="lodge-hut" alt="Mountain Hut icon" /> **Mountain Hut** | Warming hut | Small hut for mid-mountain or roadless sites. |
| <Icon name="lodge-a-frame" alt="A-Frame Day Lodge icon" /> **A-Frame Day Lodge** | Day lodge | First lodge, where guests arrive. Cheapest. Default. |
| <Icon name="lodge-chalet" alt="Chalet Lodge icon" /> **Chalet Lodge** | Base lodge | Bigger base lodge. Ski days stay full at a higher ticket price. |
| <Icon name="lodge-summit" alt="Swanky Summit Lodge icon" /> **Swanky Summit Lodge** | Mountain restaurant | Luxury restaurant on the mountain. High summer appeal when a lift or a lift-served run reaches it. |
| <Icon name="lodge-grand" alt="Swanky Grand Lodge icon" /> **Swanky Grand Lodge** | Flagship lodge | Flagship base lodge. Most skier room. |

- **Tier:** the lodge's role for the score. The hub shows your own lodges by building (*A-frame*, *Chalet 2*) and merged buildings by tier.
- **Build limit:** none.
- **Stacking:** the best lodge sets the **lodge base**. Other lodges add smaller extras, capped at **24** points.

## Merged Buildings

A park, landmark, signature or decor building of the city can be merged into the nearest ski area as a lodge tier,
without being rebuilt.

- **Merge:** hub > **Buildings** tab > **Mergeable buildings** chip. Buildings within **800 m** of a ski area's runs or lift
  stations are outlined: **green** when a lodge tier fits the lot, **gray** when the lot is too small. Select one (outlined
  or not) to open the **merge card**, pick a tier and click **Merge**.
- **Keeps its own use.** A park stays a park, a signature building keeps its renters. The tier adds its lodge score,
  skier room, staff and attraction. It is not a ski-day destination: guests still need a lodge of your own.
- **Lot size:** smallest lots: Warming hut **4** cells, Day lodge **9**, Base lodge and Mountain restaurant **16**,
  Flagship lodge **48**.
- **Not mergeable:** growables, city services other than parks, company and industrial buildings, and the mod's own buildings.
- **Distance:** it counts while within **800 m** of the area's runs or lift stations, and pays its monthly fee only then.
- **Fees:** a one-time merge fee (the tier's build cost less what the building cost) and a monthly fee (the tier's upkeep
  less a park's own upkeep). A tier change charges the difference. Unmerging keeps the building and refunds nothing.
- **Tourism:** the building's attraction combines its own with the ski area's (square root of the sum of squares). Only the
  ski area's part counts toward its share of city tourism.
- **Finding them:** the Attraction view's **Merged buildings** fold. Select a merged building to change its tier or unmerge it.

## Guest Requirements

Ski days send skiers to a lodge, so they need one. Any tier works if it meets the rules below. A lodge too far from the runs still counts toward attraction and gets tourists and residents on leisure, but no ski-day guests.

- **Road access:** needs a road. Hub line: *Lodge or school: road required*. Exempt: Mountain Hut and Swanky Summit Lodge.
- **Off-road A-Frame, Chalet or Grand:** a cat track within **25 m** of the lot, whose chain of cat tracks reaches a lodge with a road or the Ski Bus Depot of the same ski area, replaces the road and city power, water, garbage and mail: no warnings, no utility fees. Its staff move to the job host (below). It gets no guests by road and no parking count. A broken chain keeps it supplied for one game hour, so a section can be redrawn.
- **Services:** missing staff, power or water lower sign efficiency, and attraction with it. A lodge deactivated in its info panel counts **0**.
- **Steep ground:** lodges and the Ski School keep the slope they stand on. On very steep ground they sink into the hillside, and the game can flag No Pedestrian Access.
- **Distance to runs:** ski-day guests only target a lodge within **800 m** of the ski area's runs or lift stations.
- **Parking:** car spaces of any kind within **600 m**, counted per lodge with a road. Tourist parties from outside the city drive, and none arrive at a lodge with 0 spaces. The nearer the spaces, the more come. Hotel guests within **900 m** walk. A blue ring shows the 600 m when you place or select a lodge with a road. Hub line: *Lodge without parking* (*No parking: Lodge X*). The hub's **Guests** view shows each lodge's count.
- **Off-season staff:** after **12 game hours** with no open run, lodges and the Ski School keep **half** their staff (rounded up) and wages until a run opens. Staff on the job host halve too. Patrol stays full. Upkeep stays. Option *Half staff off-season*, default on.
- **Job host:** the active lodge with a road nearest the Welcome Sign, else the sign, employs the staff of the buildings without staff of their own (Hut, Summit, both patrol buildings, an off-road lodge). They take the job host's education mix.

## Guests at a Far Hill

Residents only take ski days within the longest ski-day trip (**7 km** by default, option *Longest ski-day trip*). The chance falls with distance. What helps a hill far from town:

- **Faster access roads:** the trip has to fit before closing. Measured in one test city, km per game hour: 2-lane highway **8.2**, small road **2.3**, gravel **1.8**.
- **Hotels:** only tourists staying in a hotel take ski days. Without hotels, tourist parties still come on leisure if the lodge has parking.
- **Parking at the lodge:** one far hill went from 0 tourists to about 200 a month with a single 41-space lot.
- **A [Ski Bus Depot](#ski-bus-depot) in town.**

## Ski School

<Icon name="building-school" alt="Ski School icon" /> Sells lessons and brings locals skiing more often. See [Passes and Lessons](/guides/passes-and-lessons/).

- **Score:** **+12** to *Runs, lifts and school* while at least one green run counts.
- **Demand:** locals ski **20 %** more often.
- **Limit:** one per ski area. A second school adds nothing.
- **Adds 0** with the hub status *No ski school*, *No green run*, *No run open yet* or *Deactivated*.
- **Terrain:** level the ground at the top of the magic-carpet area, then slope it down to its base.

## Ski Patrol

Two buildings: <Icon name="building-patrol-hut" alt="Ski Patrol Hut icon" /> **Ski Patrol Hut**, <Icon name="building-patrol-hq" alt="Ski Patrol HQ icon" /> **Ski Patrol HQ**.

- **Placement:** within the ski area radius of the Welcome Sign, near steep runs.
- **Effect:** black and double-black runs within **1 km** score **15 %** more.
- **Unserved runs:** within **1 km**, count at least **70 %** instead of **40 %**.

## Ski Bus Depot

<Icon name="building-bus-depot" alt="Ski Bus Depot icon" /> Few ski-day guests make a trip beyond about **7 km**, so a good hill beyond that draws almost nobody. The depot is a coach stop in town: a home closer to the depot than to any lodge starts its ski day there, so the distance that counts is home to depot. In testing, it took an out-of-reach hill from **0** ski-day guests to about **20 %** of its potential, like a hill beside town, and the hill earned about **5** times the coach bill.

- **Link:** the nearest Welcome Sign at **any distance**. It is the only building without the radius limit.
- **Requirements:** its own road, and at least one active hill lodge (a Mountain Hut or Swanky Summit Lodge counts).
- **Guests:** count as the area's guests and pay tickets, lessons and passes, but no lodge spend.
- **Skier room and score:** adds neither.
- **Coach bill:** **1,500** a month plus **300** per straight-line km to the area's nearest hill lodge, shown as *Ski bus* in the hub's Finances view. A depot that cannot work bills nothing.
- **Hub lines:** *Bus depot: road required*, *Bus depot: no hill lodge*.
- **The coach:** a SKI BUS coach drives between the depot and the nearest lodge with a road. It carries nobody: guests make the trip themselves.

## Ticket Tolerance

Ski days stay full up to a ticket price of **40** plus lodge tolerance: Chalet **+20**, Summit and Grand **+40**. Price curve: [Passes and Lessons](/guides/passes-and-lessons/).

<Aside type="tip" title="Ski Day Limit">
Hub **Guests** view names the current limit on ski days:
- **Runs:** "bigger and easier runs hold more skiers at once".
- **Lodges:** each holds a set number of skiers at once, more on a powder day.
</Aside>
