## Lodges

Guest entry point and capacity source. No lodge: attraction score **0**.

| Tier | Role |
|---|---|
| <Icon name="lodge-hut" alt="Mountain Hut icon" /> **Mountain Hut** | Small hut for mid-mountain or roadless sites. |
| <Icon name="lodge-a-frame" alt="A-Frame Day Lodge icon" /> **A-Frame Day Lodge** | Base lodge. Cheapest. Default. |
| <Icon name="lodge-chalet" alt="Chalet Lodge icon" /> **Chalet Lodge** | Larger day lodge. Ski days stay full at a higher ticket price. |
| <Icon name="lodge-summit" alt="Swanky Summit Lodge icon" /> **Swanky Summit Lodge** | Luxury summit lodge. High summer appeal when a lift reaches it. |
| <Icon name="lodge-grand" alt="Swanky Grand Lodge icon" /> **Swanky Grand Lodge** | Highest guest capacity. |

- **Build limit:** none.
- **Stacking:** the best lodge sets the **lodge base**. Other lodges add smaller extras, capped at **24** points (diminishing returns).

## Visitor Requirements

Ski days send skiers to a lodge, so they need one. Any tier works if it meets the rules below. A lodge too far from the runs still counts toward attraction and still gets tourists and residents on leisure, but no ski-day guests.

- **Road access:** deck side touches a road. Error state: *"Lodge is not at a road"*. Exempt: Mountain Hut and Swanky Summit Lodge (mountain sites, no road needed).
- **Services:** staff, power, water. Missing services lower sign efficiency, and attraction with it.
- **Distance to runs:** ski-day guests only target a lodge within **800 m** of the ski area's runs or lift stations.
- **Parking:** car spaces within **300 m** of the lodge (parking lot or street parking). Warning state: *"No parking near the lodge"*.
- **Power state:** a lodge set to Inactive in its own info panel counts **0**.
- **Off-season staff:** after **12 game hours** with no open run, lodges and the Ski School keep **half** their jobs (rounded up) until a run opens again. The jobs of the Mountain Hut and Swanky Summit Lodge, carried by the Welcome Sign, halve too. Patrol stays full. Upkeep is not reduced; setting a building Inactive cuts it to a tenth and stops its wages. Option *Half staff off-season*, default on.

## Ski School

<Icon name="building-school" alt="Ski School icon" /> Teaches lessons and lifts local skiing. See [Passes and Lessons](/guides/passes-and-lessons/).

- **Score:** **+12** to *Runs, lifts and Ski School* while at least one green run counts.
- **Demand:** locals ski **20 %** more often.
- **Status codes:** *No ski school* (none built), *No green run* (no green run counts), *No run counts yet*, *Switched off*. Each = score contribution 0.
- **Terrain:** level the ground at the top of the magic-carpet area, then use the slope tool down to its base.

## Ski Patrol

Two buildings: <Icon name="building-patrol-hut" alt="Ski Patrol Hut icon" /> **Ski Patrol Hut**, <Icon name="building-patrol-hq" alt="Ski Patrol HQ icon" /> **Ski Patrol HQ**.

- **Placement:** within **5 km** of the Welcome Sign, near steep runs.
- **Effect:** black and double-black runs within **1 km** score **15 %** more.
- **Unserved runs:** within **1 km**, count at least **70 %** instead of **40 %**.

## Ticket Tolerance

Ski days stay full up to a ticket price of **40** plus lodge tolerance.

- **Chalet:** **+20**.
- **Summit, Grand:** **+40**.
- **Price curve:** [Passes and Lessons](/guides/passes-and-lessons/).

<Aside type="tip" title="Ski Day Limit">
Hub **Guests** view names the current limit on ski days:
- **Runs:** "bigger and easier runs hold more skiers at once".
- **Lodges:** each holds a set number of skiers at once, more on a powder day.
</Aside>
