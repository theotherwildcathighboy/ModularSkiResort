## Lodges

<Shot src="lodge-day.jpg" label="A Chalet Lodge at a road, with parking in front." />

Guest entry point and capacity source. No lodge: attraction score **0**.

| Tier | Role |
|---|---|
| <Icon name="lodge-hut" alt="Mountain Hut icon" /> **Mountain Hut** | Small hut for mid-mountain or roadless sites. |
| <Icon name="lodge-a-frame" alt="A-Frame Day Lodge icon" /> **A-Frame Day Lodge** | Base lodge. Cheapest. Default. |
| <Icon name="lodge-chalet" alt="Chalet Lodge icon" /> **Chalet Lodge** | Larger day lodge. Ski days stay full at a higher ticket price. |
| <Icon name="lodge-summit" alt="Swanky Summit Lodge icon" /> **Swanky Summit Lodge** | Luxury summit lodge. High summer appeal when a lift or a lift-served run reaches it. |
| <Icon name="lodge-grand" alt="Swanky Grand Lodge icon" /> **Swanky Grand Lodge** | Highest guest capacity. |

- **Build limit:** none.
- **Stacking:** the best lodge sets the **lodge base**. Other lodges add smaller extras, capped at **24** points (diminishing returns).

## Visitor Requirements

Ski days send skiers to a lodge, so they need one. Any tier works if it meets the rules below. A lodge too far from the runs still counts toward attraction and still gets tourists and residents on leisure, but no ski-day guests.

- **Road access:** deck side touches a road. Error state: *"Lodge is not at a road"*. Exempt: Mountain Hut and Swanky Summit Lodge (mountain sites, no road needed).
- **Off-road A-Frame, Chalet or Grand:** with no road, a cat track within **25 m** of the lot whose chain of cat tracks reaches a lodge with a road or the Ski Bus Depot (same ski area) replaces city power, water, garbage and mail: no warnings and no utility fees. Its jobs move to the area's carrier (below). No guests arrive at it by road, and it has no parking count. With neither a road nor such a chain: *"Lodge is not at a road"*, the game's utility warnings and a lower sign efficiency. A broken chain keeps the lodge supplied for one game hour, so a cat track section can be redrawn.
- **Services:** staff, power, water. Missing services lower sign efficiency, and attraction with it.
- **Pipes:** highways and highway bridges carry no water or sewer pipes. A resort road cut off from town by them shows Water and Sewage notifications until a pipe joins the two networks. Gravel roads do carry pipes.
- **Steep ground:** lodges and the Ski School keep the slope they stand on. On very steep ground the building sinks into the hillside, and the game can flag No Pedestrian Access there.
- **Distance to runs:** ski-day guests only target a lodge within **800 m** of the ski area's runs or lift stations.
- **Parking:** car spaces within **300 m** of the lodge (parking lot or street parking), counted for each lodge with a road on its own. Tourist parties drive, and none arrive at a lodge with 0 spaces. Warning state: *"No parking near the lodge"*. The hub's **Guests** view shows each lodge's count.
- **Power state:** a lodge deactivated in its own info panel counts **0**.
- **Off-season staff:** after **12 game hours** with no open run, lodges and the Ski School keep **half** their jobs (rounded up) until a run opens again. The jobs of the Mountain Hut and Swanky Summit Lodge, carried by the area's carrier, halve too. Patrol stays full. Upkeep is not reduced; deactivating a building cuts it to a tenth and stops its wages. Option *Half staff off-season*, default on.
- **Carrier:** the jobs of buildings without staff of their own (Hut, Summit, both patrol buildings, an off-road lodge on a cat track chain) sit on the active lodge with a road nearest the Welcome Sign, else on the sign. Carried jobs take the carrier's education mix.

## Guests at a Far Hill

Residents only take ski days within the ski area's reach (**7 km** by default), and the chance falls with distance. What helps a hill far from town:

- **Faster access roads:** the trip has to fit before closing. Measured in one test city, in km per game hour: 2-lane highway **8.2**, small road **2.3**, gravel **1.8**.
- **Hotels:** only tourists staying in a hotel take ski days. A city without hotels still gets tourist parties on leisure, if the lodge has parking.
- **Parking at the lodge:** one far hill went from 0 tourists to about 200 a month with a single 41-space lot at its road-side lodge.
- **A [Ski Bus Depot](#ski-bus-depot) in town:** brings residents beyond that reach, for a monthly bill.

## Ski School

<Icon name="building-school" alt="Ski School icon" /> Teaches lessons and lifts local skiing. See [Passes and Lessons](/guides/passes-and-lessons/).

- **Score:** **+12** to *Runs, lifts and Ski School* while at least one green run counts.
- **Demand:** locals ski **20 %** more often.
- **Status codes:** *No ski school* (none built), *No green run* (no green run counts), *No run counts yet*, *Deactivated*. Each = score contribution 0.
- **Terrain:** level the ground at the top of the magic-carpet area, then use the slope tool down to its base.

## Ski Patrol

Two buildings: <Icon name="building-patrol-hut" alt="Ski Patrol Hut icon" /> **Ski Patrol Hut**, <Icon name="building-patrol-hq" alt="Ski Patrol HQ icon" /> **Ski Patrol HQ**.

- **Placement:** within **5 km** of the Welcome Sign, near steep runs.
- **Effect:** black and double-black runs within **1 km** score **15 %** more.
- **Unserved runs:** within **1 km**, count at least **70 %** instead of **40 %**.

## Ski Bus Depot

<Icon name="building-bus-depot" alt="Ski Bus Depot icon" /> **The problem:** ski-day guests come from homes and hotels, and few make a trip beyond about **7 km**. A good hill beyond that draws almost nobody.

**What it does:** a coach stop in town, placed from the hub **Buildings** tab, next to the lodges. A home closer to the depot than to any lodge starts its ski day at the depot, so the distance that counts is home to depot, not home to hill. In exchange you pay a monthly coach bill that grows with the distance.

In testing, a depot took a hill out of reach from **0** ski-day guests to about **20 %** of its potential guests, within the range of a hill beside town, and the hill's tickets, passes and lessons earned about **5** times the coach bill. Costs and numbers: [Buildings](/reference/buildings/#ski-bus-depot).

- **Link:** the nearest Welcome Sign at **any distance**; the only building without the **5 km** limit.
- **Requirements:** its own road, and at least one working lodge on the hill. A Mountain Hut or Swanky Summit Lodge counts. It does not need to be near the runs.
- **Distance:** a guest's reach, travel time and fit are measured from home to the depot, the same way as to a lodge.
- **Guests:** arrivals count as the area's guests and pay tickets, lessons and passes as at a lodge. No lodge spend. Leisure visitors who pick the depot count and pay too.
- **Capacity and score:** adds neither. Ski-day capacity stays set by the runs and the lodges on the hill, and the depot is in no lodge count.
- **Coach bill:** **1,500** a month plus **300** per straight-line km to the area's nearest lodge on the hill, booked in the Finances view as *Ski bus*. A depot that cannot work bills nothing.
- **Status codes:** *Bus depot without a road*, *Bus depot with no lodge on the hill*.

An oxblood coach with SKI BUS painted on it drives back and forth between the depot and the area's nearest lodge with a road. It carries nobody: it is what the bill looks like, and guests still make the trip themselves. With no road between the two, or no lodge with a road, no coach drives and the depot still works.

## Ticket Tolerance

Ski days stay full up to a ticket price of **40** plus lodge tolerance.

- **Chalet:** **+20**.
- **Summit, Grand:** **+40**.
- **Price curve:** [Passes and Lessons](/guides/passes-and-lessons/).

<Aside type="tip" title="Ski Day Limit">
Hub **Guests** view names the current limit on ski days:
- **Runs:** "bigger and easier runs hold more skiers at once".
- **Lodges:** each holds a set number of skiers at once, more on a powder day.
