## Ski Days

Mod-owned visitors: residents and hotel tourists on a day out skiing. Target: a lodge within reach of home. Payment: at the gate.

| Option (Main > Ski days) | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Locals who ski | 25 % | Residents' skiing frequency. **0** disables residents. |
| Tourists who ski | 50 % | Same, for hotel guests. |
| Longest ski-day trip | 7 km | No trips from farther than this to a lodge. Fewer trips with distance. |
| Powder pull | 100 % | Fresh-snow bonus. At **100 %** a full powder day doubles the residents who go. |

- **Increases trips:** attraction, fresh snow (a powder day is heavy snowfall in the last 24 hours), shorter distance.
- **Decreases trips:** lift ticket price above **40**.

## Lift Ticket

Paid on arrival, from household to city balance. One price for all ski areas.

- **Option:** Main > Ski area balance > Lift ticket price.
- **Default:** **80**. **Maximum:** **200**. **0:** tickets off.
- **Ski-day curve:** thins out above **40**: about **90 %** at 60, **80 %** at 80, **20 %** at 200.
- **Lodge tolerance:** chalet, summit and grand lodges raise the price where thinning starts.
- **Game-sent visitors:** arrive at any price and pay it.

## Lodge Spend

Paid by each paying ski guest at the arrival lodge. Scales with lodge tier.

- **Default setting:** **15** per guest.
- **Hut, A-frame:** **15**. **Chalet:** **23**. **Summit, Grand:** **45**.
- **Source:** household funds left after the lift ticket. Tickets off: spend **0**.
- **Setting 0:** spend off.

## Season Passes

Pre-paid ski season, July to June. Holders skip the gate fee and ski more often.

- **Purchase decision:** once per season per resident household, when a ski area first opens to it. Chance = *Season pass share* (default **50 %**) × the household's chance of skiing there.
- **Price:** *Season pass price*, default **2** lift tickets, at the ticket price on the sale date, locked for the season. Breakeven: more than **2** ski days.
- **Affordability:** no purchase into debt. A household that declines pays day tickets all season and is asked once only.
- **Tourists:** never buy a pass.
- **Pre-season sale:** first month of the season. Each household in reach rolls as above; revenue arrives as a lump. Hub label: *Season passes sold before the season*.
- **Share 0:** passes off.
- **Options:** Main > Ski area balance.

## Lessons

Requires a ski area with a working **Ski School**.

- **Share:** **30 %** of that day's skiers (default).
- **Price:** **1.5** lift tickets at the day's ticket price.
- **Payment:** household funds left after the lift ticket or pass.
- **Share 0:** lessons off.

<Aside type="note" title="Income Booking">
Tickets, passes, lessons and lodge spend book together as resort income. Economy panel location: [Economy](/guides/economy-panel/).
</Aside>
