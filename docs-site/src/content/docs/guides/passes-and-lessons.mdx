## Ski Days

Ski days are the mod's own visitors: residents and hotel tourists on a day out skiing. They go to a lodge within the longest ski-day trip of home and pay at the gate.

| Option (Options > Modular Ski Resort > Ski days) | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Locals who ski | 25 % | Residents' skiing frequency. **0** disables residents. |
| Tourists who ski | 50 % | Same, for hotel guests. |
| Longest ski-day trip | 7 km | No trips from farther than this to a lodge. Fewer trips with distance. |
| Powder pull | 100 % | Fresh-snow bonus. At **100 %** a full powder day doubles the residents who go. |

- **Increases trips:** attraction, fresh snow (a powder day is heavy snowfall in the last 24 hours), shorter distance.
- **Decreases trips:** lift ticket price above **40**, or above **60** / **80** with a better lodge (see Lift Ticket).

## Lift Ticket

Paid on arrival, from household to city balance. One price for all ski areas.

- **Option:** Options > Modular Ski Resort > Ski area balance > Lift ticket price.
- **Default:** **100**. **Maximum:** **200**. **0:** tickets off.
- **Ski-day demand:** ski days thin out above a price that depends on the ski area's best lodge. A Chalet Lodge raises it from **40** to **60**, a Swanky Summit or Grand Lodge to **80**. A merged lodge counts as its tier. Share of the ski-day guests who still come:

  | Ticket price | Hut or A-frame only | Chalet Lodge | Swanky Summit or Grand Lodge |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 40 | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % |
  | 60 | 90 % | 100 % | 100 % |
  | 80 | 80 % | 90 % | 100 % |
  | 100 | 70 % | 80 % | 90 % |
  | 120 | 60 % | 70 % | 80 % |
  | 140 | 50 % | 60 % | 70 % |
  | 160 | 40 % | 50 % | 60 % |
  | 180 | 30 % | 40 % | 50 % |
  | 200 | 20 % | 30 % | 40 % |

- **Hub:** the Finances view has a slider for this price. It is the same Option, for every ski area. One word names the demand at the area shown:
  - *unaffected:* 100 %.
  - *high:* 85 % or more.
  - *fair:* 60 % or more.
  - *low:* 40 % or more.
  - *very low:* below 40 %.

  The slider track turns from green to red with the demand.
- **Game-sent visitors:** arrive at any price and pay it.

## Lodge Spend

Paid by each paying ski guest at the arrival lodge. Scales with lodge tier.

- **Default setting:** **15** per guest.
- **Hut, A-frame:** **15**. **Chalet:** **23**. **Summit, Grand:** **45**.
- **Source:** household funds left after the lift ticket. Tickets off: spend **0**.
- **Setting 0:** spend off.

## Season Passes

Pre-paid ski season, starting 5 months before the map's winter (July for a December winter). Holders skip the gate fee and ski more often.

- **Purchase decision:** once per season per resident household, in the pre-season sale, or at its first ski day if it was not in reach then. Chance = *Season pass share* (default **50 %**) × the household's chance of skiing there × pass demand.
- **Price:** *Season pass price*, from **20** to **2,000** in steps of **20**, default **400**. Locked for the season at the sale. Needs a lift ticket price above 0.
- **Pass demand:** judged against the lift ticket price. Every would-be buyer buys up to **4** times the ticket price, fewer above:

  | Pass price | Households that still buy |
  |---|---|
  | up to 4 × the ticket | 100 % |
  | 6 × the ticket | 75 % |
  | 8 × the ticket | 50 % |
  | 10 × the ticket | 25 % |
  | 12 × the ticket or more | none |

- **From 1.0.1:** the pass price used to be set as a number of lift tickets (default 4). The first start of 1.0.2 sets the new price once from it: that number × your lift ticket price. At 4 tickets or fewer, as many households buy as before. Above 4, fewer do.
- **Hub:** the Finances view has a slider for the pass price (the same Option). The pass demand uses the same words as the ticket.
- **Affordability:** no purchase into debt. A household that declines pays day tickets all season and is asked once only.
- **Tourists:** never buy a pass.
- **Pre-season sale:** from the season's second month (August for a December winter), as a lump, once per ski area. Nothing needs to be open: households judge the resort as if every run, lodge and the Ski School were open. They need a lodge with a road near the runs, open or closed. Hub row: *Pre-season passes*.
- **Share 0:** passes off. Options: Options > Modular Ski Resort > Ski area balance.

## Lessons

Requires a ski area with a working **Ski School**.

- **Share:** **30 %** of that day's skiers (default).
- **Price:** **1.5** lift tickets at the day's ticket price.
- **Payment:** household funds left after the lift ticket or pass.
- **Share 0:** lessons off.

<Aside type="note" title="Income Booking">
Tickets, passes, lessons and lodge spend book together as resort income. Economy panel location: [Economy](/guides/economy-panel/).
</Aside>
