## Drawing

Hub **Runs** tab > type > area tool, drawn from the lift top downhill.

- **Binding:** a run within **5 km** of a Welcome Sign joins that ski area on drawing. Option **Bind runs the moment they are drawn**, default on.
- **Feedback:** slope heat-map (grey flat, green, blue, black, red double black), contour lines, tooltip with rating, hardest unavoidable pitch, length, vertical drop.

## Rating

Rating = slope of the **hardest unavoidable pitch**: the crux of the easiest line from run top to run bottom. Not the average (a flat runout cannot hide a steep pitch), not the steepest patch (one corner on a bank cannot make a double black).

| Slope of that pitch | Rating | Worth (before size and type) |
|---|---|---|
| under 6 % | Flat (unrated) | 0 |
| 6 to 25 % | Green circle | 6 |
| 25 to 40 % | Blue square | 12 |
| 40 to 60 % | Black diamond | 20 |
| 60 % and over | Double black diamond | 28 |

- **Cannot be rated:** outline too large, too thin or without terrain. Worth **3**.
- **New run:** counts **0** until rated.
- **Reshaped run:** keeps its last rating during re-rating.
- **Size factor:** **1** at 2 ha, **2** at the top end, minimum **0.25**. See [Attraction Formula](/reference/attraction/#run-value).

## Run Types

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Natural">
    **Surface:** none drawn (visible on the **piste map**). Opens on natural snow only.
    **Value:** green ×**0.5**, blue ×**0.75**, black ×**1**. **Build fee:** 500 + 250 per ha. **Upkeep:** none.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Groomed">
    **Surface:** groomed snow. **Value:** full when open.
    **Build fee:** 2,000 + 1,000 per ha. **Upkeep:** 150 per ha per month while snow-covered.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Snowmaking">
    **Surface:** groomed, plus snow guns (lances, fan guns, hydrants) where the air is cold enough.
    **Build fee:** 4,000 + 4,000 per ha. **Upkeep:** 500 per ha per month while snow-covered.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Night">
    **Surface:** snowmaking plus floodlights. **Value:** ×**1.25** of groomed.
    **Build fee:** 6,000 + 7,000 per ha. **Upkeep:** 800 per ha per month while snow-covered.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Cat track">
    **Role:** link across the hill. Adds **0** to score and capacity. **Pricing:** as groomed.
    **Slope limit:** under **25 %**; steeper is marked *Unsuitable*.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

Reshaping a run: no extra cost.

## Snow Conditions

Snow is measured per run from the game's snow simulation.

- **Open:** at least **15 cm** on **60 %** of the run. Natural black and double-black: **31 cm**.
- **Patchy:** between open and closed. Counts part of its value.
- **Closed:** snow on less than **40 %** of the run.
- **Snow guns:** snowmaking and night runs, where air temperature is at most **3.3 °C** above freezing (option *Snowmaking margin*). Higher margin: longer season, higher snowmaking bill.
- **Estimated snow:** after a save load, or for a new sign, snow is estimated from the day's temperature. Marked **≈**.
- **No open run:** ski area counts as summer sightseeing; lodges and lifts count at a reduced share.

<Aside type="note" title="Snow Gating Off">
Option **Snow & season gating** off: every run counts as open all year. Upkeep is paid only while grooming or snow guns run, so a warm map with gating off has **0** upkeep.
</Aside>

## Lift Service

- **Lift-served:** a complete, powered lift reaches the run top.
- **Backcountry:** no lift serves the run. Counts **40 %** of a served run (option *Backcountry run value*).
- **Ski patrol:** raises nearby black backcountry runs to at least **70 %**. See [Lodges, School and Patrol](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/).

## Glades

Tree tool: plans trees on and around runs. Hover a run, click.

- **Toggle:** **Ctrl+Shift+G**.
- **Tree density:** **Shift+G** steps **1** (clears the run) to **8** (thick forest).
- **Mode:** **Alt+G** switches run interior / tree line along the edge.
- **Rebinding:** all three keys in Options.

## Piste Map

Hub Runs view switch **Piste map**: draws every run on the ground in difficulty colours.

- **Natural runs:** dashed.
- **Too flat:** grey.
- **Cannot be rated:** orange.
