## Features

<CardGrid>
  <FeatureCard title="Lifts" icon="lift-detachable" alt="Detachable lift icon">
    Fixed-grip and detachable chairlifts, gondolas and an aerial tram. Chairs and cabins ride the cable.
    Tower height follows the terrain and the cable sags under the carriers.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Runs" icon="run-groomed" alt="Groomed run icon">
    Draw a run; its slope sets the rating, green to double black. Snow comes from the game's own simulation;
    snow guns extend the season where it is cold enough.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Lodges, Ski School, Patrol" icon="lodge-grand" alt="Swanky Grand Lodge icon">
    **5** lodge tiers, a Ski School and two patrol buildings, all standalone buildings. The Welcome Sign starts the ski area.
    Guests and locals ski; the city's tourism responds.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Hub" icon="glyph-history" alt="Hub History tile glyph">
    Interface for attractiveness score, runs, lifts, guests, finances, historical performance, and building tools.
  </FeatureCard>
</CardGrid>

## Install

1. Subscribe to **Modular Ski Resort** on Paradox Mods. *(The link goes here at the first upload.)*
2. **Dependencies:**
   - **Required mods:** none. Everything works on the base game.
   - **Recommended:** Move It (moves and aligns buildings and lift stations).
   - **Optional:** Tourism Overhaul, Realistic Workplaces and Households, Realistic Path Finding and other trip-planner mods. The mod detects them for labels and adjusts nothing. Those mods are maintained by others; the author does not control their updates or their compatibility.
3. **Access:** the **Ski Resort** button at the top left. Nothing is added to the game's build menus.

## Compatibility

- **Game version:** Cities: Skylines II 1.6.2f1
- **Tested with:** Move It, Tourism Overhaul, Realistic Workplaces and Households, Realistic Path Finding (unverified)
