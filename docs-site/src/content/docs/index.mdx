Draw chairlifts, gondolas and an aerial tram up your mountain, draw runs down it, and natural or artificial snow will open the runs. Guests ski (simulated, not visualized, yet), buy tickets and lessons, and the resort draws tourists to your city. No mods required, but Anarchy and Move It are highly recommended, along with Write Everywhere.

## Install

1. Subscribe to **Modular Ski Resort** on Paradox Mods. *(The link goes here at the first upload.)*
2. **Dependencies:**
   - **Required mods:** none. Everything works without other mods.
   - **Highly recommended:** Anarchy and Move It. Anarchy turns off the game's placement errors, and Move It places stations and buildings more precisely than the game's own tools.
   - **Recommended:** Write Everywhere. With it the Welcome Sign shows your ski area's name. Without it the sign keeps its plain "SKI AREA" header over a blank name board.
   - **Optional:** Tourism Overhaul, Realistic Workplaces and Households, Realistic Path Finding and other trip-planner mods. The mod detects them to show a notice in the hub and changes nothing in them. Their compatibility is up to their own authors.
3. **Access:** the **Ski Resort** button at the top left. Nothing is added to the game's build menus.

## Compatibility

- **Game version:** Cities: Skylines II 1.6.4f1
- **Partly incompatible:** Realistic Trips. It replaces the game's own leisure and citizen-behavior systems, which ski days need. With it installed, the resort sends no skiers of its own (the hub shows a notice). Lifts, runs, snow and the buildings still work.

## Known Limitations

- **Riders:** chairs and cabins move empty. Skiers are counted, not simulated.
- **Chair numbers:** towers are numbered. Chairs all show the same number.
- **Ground:** the Welcome Sign, the Bus Depot, the A-Frame and Grand lodges and the tram base level their ground, and most stations cut a level pad. The other lodges, the Ski School, both patrol buildings and the two top stations noted there keep the slope and are meant to be built into the hillside by hand. See [Ground and Slopes](/reference/buildings/#ground-and-slopes).
- **Summer:** chairlifts and gondolas stop when no run is open. The aerial tram keeps running as sightseeing.
- **Power:** a lift needs a low-voltage line at its base station. The game refuses a city high-voltage line at a lift.
- **Crossing lifts:** two lifts of the same type cannot cross. Other crossings need room from stations and towers and an angle of at least 20°. See [Lifts, Terrain](/guides/lifts/#terrain).
- **Cat tracks and lifts:** a cat track cannot pass under a lift that is already built. Build cat tracks first.
- **Roads under lifts:** where the line leaves no other choice, a tower stands on a road and the game reports an overlap.
- **Trees:** a lift fells the trees along its whole corridor, also where the rope runs high above them.
- **Far hills:** a hill far from the city's homes and hotels gets few ski-day guests. A [Ski Bus Depot](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/#ski-bus-depot) in town helps.
- **Language:** English only for now.
- **Uninstall:** first bulldoze all lifts and runs, or use **Delete all ski resort content** (Options > Modular Ski Resort > Reset), then save. Otherwise buildings and stations turn into checkered placeholder blocks. See [Uninstall](/guides/lifts/#uninstall).
- **Resort Planner:** experimental and off until turned on in Options. Its Undo covers runs only. On steep ground expect mostly black runs and few greens. See [Resort Planner](/guides/runs-and-snow/#resort-planner-experimental).

## Rough Roadmap

*(Plans, not promises. The order can change.)*

**In 1.0, the first release:**

- Four lift types, drawn runs on the game's own snow, snow guns and cat tracks.
- Five lodges, the Ski School, two patrol buildings and the Ski Bus Depot.
- The hub, the resort economy and the experimental Resort Planner.
- The [Changelog](/changelog/) has the full list.

**After the release:**

- **First two weeks:** bug reports and player feedback come first. A crash that the mod causes gets a fix before all other work.
- **Next two weeks:** UI passes and asset tune-ups, for example bullwheels that turn.

**Game updates:**

- **Park Adventures DLC (December 2, 2026):** the mod will be checked against the new game version when the DLC launches. If the update breaks a part of the mod, that fix comes first. This is a compatibility check, not a promise of DLC integration.

**Later, with no dates:**

- Summer activities, for example mountain biking and hiking trails.
- More lift types, for example T-bars and eight-seat chairlifts.
- Chair numbers, and riders on the chairs.
- Possibly Cims that ski. The travel time down a run is not solved.
