## Install

1. Subscribe to **Modular Ski Resort** on Paradox Mods. *(The link goes here at the first upload.)*
2. **Dependencies:**
   - **Required mods:** none. Everything works on the base game.
   - **Recommended:** Write Everywhere. With it the Welcome Sign shows your ski area's name. Without it the sign keeps its plain "SKI AREA" header over a blank name board.
   - **Useful:** Move It. It places stations and buildings more precisely than the game's own tools.
   - **Optional:** Tourism Overhaul, Realistic Workplaces and Households, Realistic Path Finding and other trip-planner mods. The mod detects them to show a notice in the hub and changes nothing in them. Other people maintain those mods, and the author does not control their updates or their compatibility.
3. **Access:** the **Ski Resort** button at the top left. Nothing is added to the game's build menus.

## Compatibility

- **Game version:** Cities: Skylines II 1.6.4f1
- **Partly incompatible:** Realistic Trips. It replaces the game's own leisure and citizen-behavior systems, which ski days need. With it installed, the resort sends no skiers of its own (the hub shows a notice). Lifts, runs, snow and the buildings still work.

## Known Limitations

- **Riders:** chairs and cabins move empty. Skiers are counted, not simulated.
- **Chair numbers:** towers are numbered. Chairs all show the same number.
- **Stations:** most chairlift and gondola stations cut a level pad into the slope but fill nothing, so the downhill side keeps the slope. The aerial tram's base levels its own pad. The fixed-grip top station and the aerial tram's top keep the slope as placed, so on steep ground they can sit partly in the hillside.
- **Summer:** chairlifts and gondolas stop when no run is open. The aerial tram keeps running as sightseeing.
- **Power:** a lift needs a low-voltage line at its base station. The game refuses a city high-voltage line at a lift.
- **Crossing lifts:** a lift crosses a lift of another type over or under it, where the towers can give the height the game needs. Two lifts of the same type cannot cross. No crossing is built near a station, beside a tower of the other lift, or at an angle under 20°. See [Lifts, Terrain](/guides/lifts/#terrain).
- **Cat tracks and lifts:** a cat track cannot pass under a lift that is already built. Build cat tracks first, then the lifts over them.
- **Roads under lifts:** towers stand clear of roads, tracks and paths where the line allows it. They do not stand clear of the roads and paths inside a building's lot. Where the line does not allow it, the game reports the tower as overlapping. Move the road or the tower.
- **Trees:** a lift fells the trees along its whole corridor, also where the rope runs high above them.
- **Far hills:** a hill far from the city's homes and hotels gets few ski-day guests. The hub shows the distance. A [Ski Bus Depot](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/#ski-bus-depot) in town helps.
- **Language:** English only for now.
- **Uninstall:** first bulldoze all lifts and runs, or use **Delete all ski resort content** (Options > Modular Ski Resort > Reset), then save. Without that, buildings and stations turn into checkered placeholder blocks. Runs stay as flat patches. See [Uninstall](/guides/lifts/#uninstall).
- **Resort Planner:** experimental. It may change, and it is off until you turn it on in Options. It proposes runs, cat tracks and lifts. **Build** makes the cat tracks and runs. **Build lifts** then adds the lifts one at a time. A lift line the planner cannot fit gets its stations only, and the lift tool opens on it for you to place the towers. Its Undo covers runs only and ends when the lift build starts. On steep ground expect mostly black runs and few greens. See [Runs and Snow](/guides/runs-and-snow/#resort-planner-experimental).

## Rough Roadmap

*(Plans, not promises. The order can change.)*

**In 1.0, the first release:**

- Four lift types, drawn runs on the game's own snow, snow guns and cat tracks.
- Five lodges, the Ski School, two patrol buildings and the Ski Bus Depot.
- The hub, the resort economy and the experimental Resort Planner.
- The [Changelog](/changelog/) has the full list.

**After the release:**

- **First two weeks:** bug reports and player feedback come first. A crash that the mod causes gets a fix before all other work.
- **Next two weeks:** UI passes and asset tune-ups, for example bullwheels that turn.

**Game updates:**

- **Park Adventures DLC (December 2, 2026):** the mod will be checked against the new game version when the DLC launches. If the update breaks a part of the mod, that fix comes first. This is a compatibility check, not a promise of DLC integration.

**Later, with no dates:**

- Summer activities, for example mountain biking and hiking trails.
- More lift types, for example T-bars and eight-seat chairlifts.
- Chair numbers, and riders on the chairs.
- Possibly Cims that ski. The travel time down a run is not solved.
