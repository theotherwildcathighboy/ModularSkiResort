## The Loop

**Welcome Sign → lifts → runs → any lodge.** That is all you need for a working resort.

1. Place the Welcome Sign by a road.
2. Build a lift, from base station to top station. A run counts in full only while a lift serves it.
3. Draw a run under the lift.
4. Place any lodge within 800 m of the runs or lift stations.

Everything else is for more, and for fun: Ski School, ski patrol, snow guns, lit runs for night skiing, cat tracks and grading, the
hub's economy and history views.

## Step Details

One small resort from empty mountain to opening day. Place the Welcome Sign first. It starts the ski area.

<Aside type="tip" title="Site Selection">
Snow decides viability. Option **Show the winter snow line while placing** (default on) draws two lines on the
terrain while placing a sign or drawing a run:
- **Average winter snow line.**
- **Snow-gun line:** fainter, below the first line.

Build on or above both.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Place the Welcome Sign.**

   Hub (top-left button) > **Place Welcome Sign**.

   - **Role:** the ski area. Lifts and runs within its radius (**5 km** by default) belong to it.
   - **Site check:** while placing, a ring shows the radius and the tooltip gives the height under the cursor, the lowest and highest dry ground in the ring, the vertical above the winter snow line (the skiable vertical), and how much of the ground lies above that line.
   - **Placement:** front on a road, snapped. It stands 8 m back from the road on leveled ground.
   - **Moving it:** **Relocate** in the sign's info panel. Its runs stay linked. Bulldozing a sign deletes every run linked to it, with the runs' glade trees, and refunds no run fee. A warning shows the run count and asks first.
   - **Effect:** sign efficiency multiplies the attraction score.

   <Shot src="start-card.jpg" label="The hub's Start a ski area card with its four steps, and the Welcome Sign being placed at a road." />

2. **Build the chairlift.**

   Lifts are required. A run reaches full value only while a lift serves it. Without one, runs count as backcountry
   (**40 %** by default). Which runs a lift serves, and the distances:
   [Runs and Snow, Lift Service](/guides/runs-and-snow/#lift-service).

   Hub > **Lifts** tab > lift type, then:

   1. Place the **base station** at the bottom.
   2. Place the **top station** at the top, facing the base station. A guide line shows when they are straight.
   3. Open the **lift tool** (the line tile, or **Ctrl+Shift+L**), click the base station, then the top station. Click the line to add a tower, drag a tower to move it, and press **Enter** to build. On steep ground, draw one section at a time. You can redo a single section later.
   4. Connect the base station to a **low-voltage power line**. No power, no lift.

   <Shot src="first-lift.jpg" label="A lift line being drawn between two stations: the preview with its towers, the hub's Lifts view and its Lifts tab." />

   Details: [Lifts](/guides/lifts/).

3. **Draw a run.**

   Hub > **Runs** tab > **Run** tile. Draw the outline from the lift top downhill, inside the sign's ring. The rows above the run list set its features. **Groomed** on is enough to start.

   - **Rating:** green, blue, black or double black from the hardest unavoidable pitch. Shown in the tooltip while drawing.
   - **Full value:** requires a running lift that serves the run.

   <Shot src="run-draw.jpg" label="A run outline being drawn, with the slope heat-map and the rating tooltip." />

   Details: [Runs and Snow](/guides/runs-and-snow/).

4. **Add a lodge.**

   Hub > **Buildings** tab > lodge, within **800 m** of the runs or lift stations and inside the sign's ring. Any lodge works: ski-day guests only come to a lodge within that distance.

   - **Default:** A-Frame Day Lodge, the cheapest lodge with real skier room.
   - **Requirements:** deck side on a road, staff, power and water. Mountain Hut and Swanky Summit Lodge need no road. An A-Frame, Chalet or Grand also works off-road on a cat track chain to a lodge with a road.
   - **No lodge:** attraction score **0** and no ski days.
   - **Already have a park or landmark near the runs?** The **Mergeable buildings** chip on the same tab outlines the city buildings that can join the ski area as a lodge tier. Select one to merge it. It adds score and skier room, but ski-day guests still need a lodge of your own. See [Merged Buildings](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/#merged-buildings).

   Tiers: [Lodges, Ski School and Patrol](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/).

5. **Open for the season.**

   - **Open:** at least **91 cm** of snow covers **60 %** of the run. Once open, it stays open while at least **30 cm** of snow covers at least **40 %** of it. Hub status line: *Open* once every run is open, *Partly open* before that. The run counts are in its tooltip.
   - **Tiles:** Attraction rises first. Guests and Finances fill as ski-day trips arrive.

   <Shot src="hub-tiles.jpg" label="The hub's header, status line, problem line and tiles on an open ski area." />

</Steps>

## Problems

Hub problem line: the worst problem, such as *No lift yet* or *Lodge or school: road required*, with a button for the others.

- **Click:** flies to the cause.
- **Hover:** says why it matters.
- **No problem shown, nothing happens:** [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting/).
