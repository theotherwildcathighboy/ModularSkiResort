## The Loop

**Welcome Sign → lifts → runs → any lodge.** That is literally all you need for a working resort.

1. Place the Welcome Sign by a road.
2. Build a lift, from base station to top station. Runs only work with a lift serving them.
3. Draw a run under the lift.
4. Place any lodge within 800 m of the runs or lift stations.

Everything else is for more, and for fun: Ski School, ski patrol, snowmaking, night runs, cat tracks and grading, the
hub's economy and history views.

## Step Details

One small resort from empty mountain to opening day. Place the Welcome Sign first; it starts the ski area.

<Aside type="tip" title="Site Selection">
Snow decides viability. Option **Show the winter snow line while placing** (default on) draws two lines on the
terrain while placing a sign or drawing a run:
- **Average winter snow line.**
- **Snow-gun line:** fainter, below the first line.

Build on or above both.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Place the Welcome Sign.**

   Hub (top-left button) > **Place Welcome Sign**.

   - **Role:** the ski area. Lifts and runs within **5 km** belong to it.
   - **Placement:** front on a road, snapped. It stands 8 m back from the road on leveled ground.
   - **Moving it:** **Relocate** in the sign's info panel; its runs stay linked. Bulldozing a sign deletes every run linked to it, with the runs' glade trees, and refunds no run fee; a warning shows the run count and asks first.
   - **Effect:** sign efficiency multiplies the attraction score.

   <Shot src="start-card.jpg" label="The hub's Start a ski area card with its four steps, and the Welcome Sign being placed at a road." />

2. **Build the chairlift.**

   Lifts are required: a run reaches full value only while a lift serves it. Without one, runs count as backcountry
   (**40 %** by default). Which runs a lift serves, and the distances:
   [Runs and Snow, Lift Service](/guides/runs-and-snow/#lift-service).

   Hub > **Lifts** tab > lift type, then:

   1. Place the **base station** at the bottom.
   2. Place the **top station** at the top, facing the base station. A guide line shows when they are straight.
   3. Open the **lift tool** (the line tile, or **Ctrl+Shift+L**), click the base station, then the top station. Click the line to add a tower, drag a tower to move it, press **Enter** to build. On steep ground, draw one section at a time; a single section can be redone later.
   4. Connect the base station to a **low-voltage power line**. No power, no lift.

   <Shot src="first-lift.jpg" label="Drawing another lift line from a placed base station, with the straight-line guide and the hub's Lifts tab." />

   Details: [Lifts](/guides/lifts/).

3. **Draw a run.**

   Hub > **Runs** tab > type (**Groomed** to start). Draw the outline from the lift top downhill, within **5 km** of the sign.

   - **Rating:** green, blue, black or double black from the hardest unavoidable pitch. Shown in the tooltip while drawing.
   - **Full value:** requires a running lift that serves the run.

   <Shot src="run-draw.jpg" label="A run outline being drawn, with the slope heat-map and the rating tooltip." />

   Details: [Runs and Snow](/guides/runs-and-snow/).

4. **Add a lodge.**

   Hub > **Buildings** tab > lodge, within **800 m** of the runs or lift stations and **5 km** of the sign. Any lodge works: ski-day guests only come to a lodge within that distance.

   - **Default:** A-Frame Day Lodge, the cheapest lodge with real capacity.
   - **Requirements:** deck side on a road (Mountain Hut and Swanky Summit Lodge need none); staff, power, water.
   - **No lodge:** attraction score **0** and no ski days.

   Tiers: [Lodges, Ski School and Patrol](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/).

5. **Open for the season.**

   - **Open:** at least **91 cm** of snow covers **60 %** of the run. Hub status line: *Open · 2 of 3 runs*.
   - **Tiles:** Attraction rises first; Guests and Finances fill as ski-day trips arrive.

   <Shot src="hub-tiles.jpg" label="The hub's status line and tiles in winter: Open, attraction score, guests and finances." />

</Steps>

## Problems

Hub problem band: facts such as *No lift yet*, *Lodge is not at a road*.

- **Click:** flies to the cause.
- **Hover:** shows the cost.
- **Band empty, nothing happens:** [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting/).
