## Welcome Sign

The ski area. Runs, lifts and buildings within **5 km** belong to it (the nearest sign wins). Every lodge, the Ski School and the patrol buildings are standalone buildings, not sign upgrades.

| | |
|---|---|
| Icon | <Icon name="building-welcome-sign" alt="Welcome Sign icon" /> |
| Name | Ski Area Welcome Sign; Ski Area Welcome Sign, Angled (the same sign turned about 25° on its lot) |
| Cost | 12,000 |
| Lot | 2 x 2 cells; the sign stands about 8 m back from the road, on leveled ground |
| Requirements | Road at its front: place it snapped to a road (the setback is built in; for an angle, place the angled sign) |

## Lodges

Guest entry points; capacity and score source.

| Lodge | Build | Upkeep / month | Jobs | Base / extra | Holds at once | Ticket tolerance | Spend |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <Icon name="lodge-hut" alt="Mountain Hut icon" /> Mountain Hut | 50,000 | 3,000 | 4 | 16 / 4 | 200 | +0 | ×1 |
| <Icon name="lodge-a-frame" alt="A-Frame Day Lodge icon" /> A-Frame Day Lodge | 40,000 | 3,000 | 4 | 20 / 4 | 360 | +0 | ×1 |
| <Icon name="lodge-chalet" alt="Chalet Lodge icon" /> Chalet Lodge | 200,000 | 8,000 | 12 | 30 / 8 | 720 | +20 | ×1.5 |
| <Icon name="lodge-summit" alt="Swanky Summit Lodge icon" /> Swanky Summit Lodge | 1,500,000 | 18,000 | 8 | 40 / 12 | 480 | +40 | ×3 |
| <Icon name="lodge-grand" alt="Swanky Grand Lodge icon" /> Swanky Grand Lodge | 1,200,000 | 20,000 | 18 | 40 / 12 | 1,080 | +40 | ×3 |

- **Base / extra:** score points when best lodge (base) or any other lodge (extra). Extras capped at **24**. See [Attraction Formula](/reference/attraction/#lodge-base).
- **Holds at once:** ski-day skiers accepted at once (more on a powder day).
- **Ticket tolerance:** ski days stay full up to a lift ticket price of **40** plus this.
- **Spend:** multiplies *Lodge spend per guest* (default **15**): **15**, **23** or **45** per paying guest.
- **Road:** not required for Mountain Hut and Swanky Summit Lodge. Those two and both patrol buildings have no staff of their own: their jobs (Hut 4, Summit 8, patrol 2 each) sit on the linked Welcome Sign.
- **Off-season:** with no open run for 12 game hours, lodges and the Ski School keep half their jobs (rounded up) until a run opens. Upkeep is not reduced. Option *Half staff off-season*.
- **Summer share of score:** **0.5** (Grand **0.6**). Hut **0.75** and Summit **1.0** when a working lift top station is within **300 m**.

## Runs

Area-priced.

| Type | Build fee | Upkeep / hectare / month | Score factor |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Icon name="run-natural" alt="Natural run icon" /> Natural | 500 + 250 / ha | 0 | green ×0.5, blue ×0.75, black ×1 |
| <Icon name="run-groomed" alt="Groomed run icon" /> Groomed | 2,000 + 1,000 / ha | 150 | ×1 |
| <Icon name="run-snowmaking" alt="Snowmaking run icon" /> Snowmaking | 4,000 + 4,000 / ha | 500 | ×1 |
| <Icon name="run-night" alt="Night run icon" /> Night | 6,000 + 7,000 / ha | 800 | ×1.25 |
| <Icon name="run-cat-track" alt="Cat track icon" /> Cat track | 2,000 + 1,000 / ha | 150 | green ×0.25 |

- **Upkeep:** paid only while the run has snow (groomed, or made by guns).
- **Skiers on the slope at once, per hectare:** green **45**, blue **25**, black **15**, double black **7.5**. Natural runs: half.
