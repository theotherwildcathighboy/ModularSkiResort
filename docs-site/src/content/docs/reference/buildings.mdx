## Welcome Sign

The ski area. Runs, lifts and buildings within its radius (**5 km** by default, option **Ski area radius**) belong to it (the nearest sign wins).

| | |
|---|---|
| Icon | <Icon name="building-welcome-sign" alt="Welcome Sign icon" /> |
| Name | Ski Area Welcome Sign |
| Cost | 12,000 |
| Lot | 2 x 2 cells. The sign stands about 8 m back from the road, on leveled ground. |
| Requirements | Road at its front: place it snapped to a road (the setback is built in). |
| Move | **Relocate** in its info panel. Its runs stay linked. Bulldozing it deletes every run linked to it, with their glade trees, and refunds no run fee. |

## Lodges

Guest entry points. Source of skier room and score.

| | <Icon name="lodge-hut" alt="Mountain Hut icon" /> Mountain Hut | <Icon name="lodge-a-frame" alt="A-Frame Day Lodge icon" /> A-Frame Day Lodge | <Icon name="lodge-chalet" alt="Chalet Lodge icon" /> Chalet Lodge | <Icon name="lodge-summit" alt="Swanky Summit Lodge icon" /> Swanky Summit Lodge | <Icon name="lodge-grand" alt="Swanky Grand Lodge icon" /> Swanky Grand Lodge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Build | 50,000 | 40,000 | 200,000 | 1,500,000 | 1,200,000 |
| Upkeep / month | 3,000 | 3,000 | 8,000 | 18,000 | 20,000 |
| Staff | 4 | 4 | 12 | 8 | 18 |
| Base / extra | 16 / 4 | 20 / 4 | 30 / 8 | 40 / 12 | 40 / 12 |
| Holds at once | 200 | 360 | 720 | 480 | 1,080 |
| Ticket tolerance | +0 | +0 | +20 | +40 | +40 |
| Spend per guest | 15 | 15 | 23 | 45 | 45 |
| Summer share | 0.5, near a lift 0.75 | 0.5 | 0.5 | 0.5, near a lift 1.0 | 0.6 |

- **Base / extra:** score points when best lodge (base) or any other lodge (extra). Extras capped at **24**. See [Attraction Formula](/reference/attraction/#lodge-base).
- **Holds at once:** ski-day skiers accepted at once (more on a powder day).
- **Ticket tolerance:** ski days stay full up to a lift ticket price of **40** plus this.
- **Spend per guest:** what each paying guest spends at the lodge at the default *Lodge spend per guest* of **15**. The option scales every lodge: a chalet ×1.5, a summit or grand lodge ×3.
- **Road:** not required for Mountain Hut and Swanky Summit Lodge. An A-Frame, Chalet or Grand works off-road on a cat track chain. The Hut, the Summit, both patrol buildings and off-road lodges employ their staff on the area's job host. Rules: [Guest Requirements](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/#guest-requirements), also for the off-season half staff.
- **Summer share:** the part of the lodge's score it keeps in summer. *Near a lift:* a working lift top station within **300 m**, or a lift-served run within **100 m**.

## Ground and Slopes

Each building handles a slope in one of three ways. A building that keeps the slope is meant to be built into the hillside:
fit it by hand with the terrain tools (cut a little on the uphill side, fill on the downhill side) or with Move It.

| Building | Ground | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ski Area Welcome Sign, Ski Bus Depot | Levels its lot (cut and fill) | |
| A-Frame Day Lodge, Swanky Grand Lodge | Levels its lot (cut and fill) | On a slope this leaves a cut uphill and a bank downhill. Gentle ground looks best. |
| Chalet Lodge, Mountain Hut, Swanky Summit Lodge | Keeps the slope | Built into the hillside. Each floor and deck should meet its own ground line. |
| Ski Patrol Hut, Ski Patrol HQ | Keeps the slope | Built into the hillside. |
| Ski School | Keeps the slope | The most demanding fit: level the ground at the top of the magic-carpet area, then slope it down to its base. On very steep ground it sinks, and the game can flag No Pedestrian Access. |
| Detachable quad and gondola stations, fixed-grip base station | Cuts a level pad, fills nothing | A base station cuts 12 m past its lot on the line side. The slope stays below the pad on the downhill side. |
| Aerial tram base terminal | Levels its pad (cut and fill) | |
| Fixed-grip top station, aerial tram top terminal | Keeps the slope | Built into the hillside. A top station belongs on the crest, not past it. |

## Runs

Runs are priced by area and by feature. A run pays the natural fee, and each feature it has adds its step, charged once (see [Run Features](/guides/runs-and-snow/#run-features)).

| Feature | Build fee | Upkeep / hectare / month | Score factor |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Icon name="run-natural" alt="Natural run icon" /> Natural (Groomed off) | 500 + 250 / ha | 0 | green ×0.5, blue ×0.75, black ×1 |
| <Icon name="run-groomed" alt="Groomed run icon" /> Groomed | +1,500 + 750 / ha (2,000 + 1,000 in all) | 150 | ×1 |
| <Icon name="run-snowmaking" alt="Snow guns icon" /> Snow guns | +2,000 + 3,000 / ha | +350 | ×1 |
| <Icon name="run-night" alt="Lights icon" /> Lights | +2,000 + 3,000 / ha | +300 | ×1.25 |
| <Icon name="run-cat-track" alt="Cat track icon" /> Cat track | 2,000 + 1,000 / ha | 500 while its guns can run. +300 lit in its season | green ×0.25 |

- **Upkeep:** grooming and lights while the run has snow (also while its guns are stopped because the snow is deep enough). Snow guns only where they make snow. A cat track by its plan area.
- **Skiers on the slope at once, per hectare:**

  | | Green | Blue | Black | Double black |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Groomed | 45 | 25 | 15 | 7.5 |
  | Natural | 22.5 | 12.5 | 7.5 | 3.75 |
