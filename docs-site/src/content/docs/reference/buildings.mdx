## Welcome Sign

The ski area. Runs, lifts and buildings within its radius (**5 km** by default, option **Ski area radius**) belong to it (the nearest sign wins). Every lodge, the Ski School and the patrol buildings are standalone buildings. They are not sign upgrades.

| | |
|---|---|
| Icon | <Icon name="building-welcome-sign" alt="Welcome Sign icon" /> |
| Name | Ski Area Welcome Sign |
| Cost | 12,000 |
| Lot | 2 x 2 cells. The sign stands about 8 m back from the road, on leveled ground. |
| Requirements | Road at its front: place it snapped to a road (the setback is built in). |
| Move | **Relocate** in its info panel. Its runs stay linked. Bulldozing it deletes every run linked to it, with the runs' glade trees. Run fees are not refunded. |

## Lodges

Guest entry points. Source of skier room and score.

| Lodge | Build | Upkeep / month | Staff | Base / extra | Holds at once | Ticket tolerance | Spend |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <Icon name="lodge-hut" alt="Mountain Hut icon" /> Mountain Hut | 50,000 | 3,000 | 4 | 16 / 4 | 200 | +0 | ×1 |
| <Icon name="lodge-a-frame" alt="A-Frame Day Lodge icon" /> A-Frame Day Lodge | 40,000 | 3,000 | 4 | 20 / 4 | 360 | +0 | ×1 |
| <Icon name="lodge-chalet" alt="Chalet Lodge icon" /> Chalet Lodge | 200,000 | 8,000 | 12 | 30 / 8 | 720 | +20 | ×1.5 |
| <Icon name="lodge-summit" alt="Swanky Summit Lodge icon" /> Swanky Summit Lodge | 1,500,000 | 18,000 | 8 | 40 / 12 | 480 | +40 | ×3 |
| <Icon name="lodge-grand" alt="Swanky Grand Lodge icon" /> Swanky Grand Lodge | 1,200,000 | 20,000 | 18 | 40 / 12 | 1,080 | +40 | ×3 |

- **Base / extra:** score points when best lodge (base) or any other lodge (extra). Extras capped at **24**. See [Attraction Formula](/reference/attraction/#lodge-base).
- **Holds at once:** ski-day skiers accepted at once (more on a powder day).
- **Ticket tolerance:** ski days stay full up to a lift ticket price of **40** plus this.
- **Spend:** multiplies *Lodge spend per guest* (default **15**): **15**, **23** or **45** per paying guest.
- **Road:** not required for Mountain Hut and Swanky Summit Lodge. A-Frame, Chalet and Grand work off-road with a cat track within **25 m** chained to a lodge with a road or the Ski Bus Depot. They get no city utilities, no guests by road and no parking count. Those off-road lodges, the Hut, the Summit and both patrol buildings have no staff of their own. Their staff (Hut 4, Summit 8, patrol 2 each, an off-road lodge its own) sit on the job host. The job host is the area's active lodge with a road nearest the Welcome Sign, else the sign.
- **Off-season:** with no open run for 12 game hours, lodges and the Ski School keep half their staff (rounded up) until a run opens. Upkeep is not reduced. Option *Half staff off-season*.
- **Summer share of score:** **0.5** (Grand **0.6**). Hut **0.75** and Summit **1.0** when a working lift top station is within **300 m**, or a lift-served run within **100 m**.

## Ski Bus Depot

A ski-day target in town for a far hill. It adds no skier room and no score. [What it does](/guides/lodges-school-patrol/#ski-bus-depot).

| | |
|---|---|
| Icon | <Icon name="building-bus-depot" alt="Ski Bus Depot icon" /> |
| Cost / upkeep / staff | 60,000 / 2,000 a month / 0 |
| Coach bill | 1,500 a month + 300 per straight-line km to the area's nearest hill lodge, per depot. It shows in the hub's Finances view, not in the Economy panel |
| Link | Nearest Welcome Sign at any distance |
| Road | Required |
| Needs | At least one working hill lodge (a Mountain Hut or Swanky Summit Lodge counts) |
| Build limit | None |
| Lot | 2 x 1 cells, on its own leveled pad |

## Runs

Runs are priced by area and by feature. A run pays the natural fee, and each feature it has adds its step, charged once (see [Run Features](/guides/runs-and-snow/#run-features)).

| Feature | Build fee | Upkeep / hectare / month | Score factor |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Icon name="run-natural" alt="Natural run icon" /> Natural (Groomed off) | 500 + 250 / ha | 0 | green ×0.5, blue ×0.75, black ×1 |
| <Icon name="run-groomed" alt="Groomed run icon" /> Groomed | +1,500 + 750 / ha (2,000 + 1,000 in all) | 150 | ×1 |
| <Icon name="run-snowmaking" alt="Snow guns icon" /> Snow guns | +2,000 + 3,000 / ha | +350 | ×1 |
| <Icon name="run-night" alt="Lights icon" /> Lights | +2,000 + 3,000 / ha | +300 | ×1.25 |
| <Icon name="run-cat-track" alt="Cat track icon" /> Cat track | 2,000 + 1,000 / ha | 500 while its guns can run. +300 lit in its season | green ×0.25 |

- **Upkeep:** grooming and lights while the run has snow (also while its guns are stopped because the snow is deep enough). Snow guns only where they make snow. A cat track by its plan area while its guns can run, plus lights by its plan area while lit in its snow season.
- **Skiers on the slope at once, per hectare:** green **45**, blue **25**, black **15**, double black **7.5**. Natural runs: half.
