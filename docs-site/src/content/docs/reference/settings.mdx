**Location:** Options > Modular Ski Resort (one page, no tabs). **Money:** game currency units.

## Hub

Hub button and auto-open behavior.

| Option | Default | |
|---|---|---|
| Show the Ski Resort button (top left) | On | Shows the hub button at the top left. The hub is always in the Universal Mod Panel too. |
| Open the hub when a Welcome Sign is selected | On | Never closes the hub. The hub closes with its X or Escape. |

## Ski Area Balance

Economy and attraction tuning.

| Option | Range | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ski area radius (km) | 2 to 10, step 0.5 | 5 | How far a ski area reaches from its Welcome Sign. Lifts and buildings follow a change. A run keeps the sign it linked to when drawn. |
| Attraction scale (K) | 1 to 10 | 4 | How much runs and lifts add to the attraction score. See [Attraction Formula](/reference/attraction/). |
| Backcountry run value | 0 to 100 %, step 5 | 40 % | What a run no running lift serves is worth, against a lift-served one. |
| Lift ticket price | 0 to 200, step 5 | 80 | What each guest pays on arrival, one price for every ski area. Above 40 it lowers ski days. 0 turns tickets off. |
| Lodge spend per guest | 0 to 50, step 5 | 15 | What each paying guest also spends at the lodge. Bigger lodges get more: ×1 at a hut or A-frame, ×1.5 at a chalet, ×3 at a summit or grand lodge. 0 turns it off. |
| Lesson share | 0 to 50 %, step 5 | 30 % | The share of skiers who also take a lesson at a ski area with a working Ski School. 0 turns lessons off. |
| Lesson price (× lift ticket) | 1 to 3, step 0.5 | 1.5 | A lesson costs this many lift tickets. Needs a ticket price above 0. |
| Season pass share | 0 to 100 %, step 5 | 50 % | The share of resident households that buy a pass. Tourists never do. 0 turns passes off. |
| Season pass price (× lift ticket) | 2 to 10 | 2 | A pass costs this many lift tickets, locked for the season. Needs a ticket price above 0. |
| Half staff off-season | On/off | On | After 12 game hours with no open run in a ski area, its lodges and Ski School keep half their staff and pay half the wages. This lasts until a run opens. Upkeep stays. |

## Ski Days

Mod-owned visitor stream (see [Passes and Lessons](/guides/passes-and-lessons/)).

| Option | Range | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Locals who ski | 0 to 50 %, step 5 | 25 % | How often residents spend a day skiing. |
| Tourists who ski | 0 to 100 %, step 5 | 50 % | How often hotel guests do. |
| Longest ski-day trip (km) | 2 to 12 | 7 | Nobody farther from a lodge, in a straight line, is sent. |
| Powder pull | 0 to 200 %, step 10 | 100 % | How much a fresh-snow day adds. At 100 %, a full powder day doubles the residents who go. |

## Snow

Open-state gating and snow-gun range.

| Option | Range | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snow & season gating | On/off | On | A run adds to the score only while snow lies on it. Off: every run counts as open all year, for maps without winter. |
| Snowmaking margin (°C) | 0 to 6, step 0.1 | 3.3: guns run in air up to 3.3 °C (38 °F) | Snow guns on snowmaking runs, night runs and cat tracks work where the air on the run is at most this many degrees above freezing. They work from 2 months before winter to 1 month after, until the run has 91 cm (3 ft) of snow. |

## Runs

Run drawing feedback and cosmetics.

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Link runs the moment they are drawn | On | A run drawn within the ski area radius of a Welcome Sign links to that ski area at once and pays its fee, also while paused. |
| Show run difficulty while drawing | On | Slope heat-map and a tooltip with rating, pitch, length and drop. |
| Contour lines while drawing runs | On | Terrain contours switch on while a run is selected in the area tool, then off again. |
| Show the winter snow line while placing | On | Two lines on the terrain: the average winter snow line, and a fainter one below where snow guns start to work. The tooltip adds the height under the cursor. While you place a sign, it also adds the lowest and highest dry ground within the radius, the vertical above the snow line and the share of ground above it. |
| Show the ski area radius | On | A ring on the ground: around the cursor while placing a Welcome Sign, around a selected sign, and around every sign while placing a run, lift or resort building. A blue ring shows the lodge parking radius (**600 m**) around an A-Frame, Chalet or Grand lodge being placed or selected. It is fainter around the other lodges with a road. |
| Show run names on the hill | On | Each run's name written on the snow along the run, in its difficulty color, at piste-map distance. |
| Run outline width | 0 to 16, step 1, default 8 | Thickness of the outline and corner dots while you draw, edit or bulldoze a run: 0.3 m (1 ft) a step, so 8 = 2.4 m (8 ft). 0 keeps the game's thin outline. |
| Snow-gun plumes | On | A vapor plume while the guns make snow. Look only. |
| Snow groomers | On | One snow groomer drives each linked cat track while the track is open. Look only: snow, rating and upkeep do not change. |
| Cat track: max grade | 5 to 25 %, step 1, default 25 % | The steepest a cat track may climb. A track that cannot stay under it turns red and asks for more length (a switchback). |
| Cat track: width (m) | 8 to 32, step 1, default 20 | Width of the cat track: the flat top the tool cuts and fills, and the run built on it. |
| Cat track: cut slope | 1 to 3, step 0.5, default 1.5 | Meters out per meter down where the track cuts into the hill. Lower is steeper. |
| Cat track: fill slope | 1 to 4, step 0.5, default 2 | Meters out per meter up where the track is built up. Lower is steeper. |
| Resort Planner (experimental) | Off | Outline a mountainside and the planner proposes lifts, cat tracks and a set of linked runs. You review and change the plan. Build makes the cat tracks and runs together. The lifts follow one after the other. An optional tree filler plants trees. Off: the tool cannot open, its key does nothing, its hub button is grayed. See [Runs and Snow](/guides/runs-and-snow/#resort-planner-experimental). |
| Cat track: constant grade | Off | Off: the track follows the ground and moves as little earth as the max grade allows. On: one even slope from end to end. |

## Lifts

Placement aids.

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Lift building aids | On | Shows a guide line and an offset readout while you place a station. The bulldozer warns what a click on a lift removes. |
| Plan lift towers | On | After you draw or change a lift line, the mod raises its towers and moves them along the line. Chairs and cabins then stay at least 3 m above the ground (trams 5 m). Off: the towers stay where the game puts them. |
| Animated lift carriers | On | Grips follow the slope of the rope, and chairs and cabins swing in the wind. Off: they hang level and still. |

## Key Bindings

Tools and diagnostics.

| Action | Default |
|---|---|
| Tree tool: toggle | Ctrl+Shift+G |
| Tree tool: next tree density (1-8) | Shift+G |
| Tree tool: interior / tree line | Alt+G |
| Lift-line tool: toggle | Ctrl+Shift+L |
| Lift-line tool: build | Enter. Only while the lift-line, cat track or Resort Planner tool is open. |
| Cat track tool: toggle | Ctrl+Shift+K |
| Cat track tool: delete mode | Ctrl+Shift+D. Only while the cat track tool is open. |
| Resort Planner: toggle | Ctrl+Shift+Q. Inert while the Resort Planner is off. |
| Dump diagnostics | Ctrl+Alt+G. Writes the drawing-feedback and tree-tool state to the mod log. It is a support tool. Needs Measurement diagnostics on. |

## Diagnostics

Field-test tooling, and the bug-report copy.

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Measurement diagnostics | Off | Extra measurements for field tests that cost a little frame time: a daily ski-day measurement pass and the Ctrl+Alt+G slope capture file. Leave off for normal play. |
| Copy diagnostics | (button) | Copies a short report to the clipboard for a bug report: mod and game versions, the computer, the options changed from their defaults, the code mods loaded, where the mod log is, and its newest error lines. The Windows user folder is replaced by `%USERPROFILE%`. |

## Reset

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Reset all options to defaults | Restores every option above and every key binding to its default, after a confirmation. |
| Delete all ski resort content | Deletes everything the mod placed in the loaded city, after a confirmation: lifts with their towers, stations, lodges, the Ski School, patrol buildings, Welcome Signs, runs, cat tracks, safety fences, and the trees the tree tool or the Resort Planner planted. **Use:** before uninstalling (delete, save, then uninstall). **No undo:** keep a save from before. **Untouched:** player power lines, roads and buildings. **Left as is:** ground graded for cat tracks, felled trees. Snow on the runs melts with the weather. A power line ending at a base station: bulldoze that last span first. Disabled outside a loaded city. |
