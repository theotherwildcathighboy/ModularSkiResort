One author (direction, verdicts, Editor imports, all in-game testing) and Claude as lead architect (design, correctness, written record). Project start: **Aug 3, 2026**.

- **Outcomes:** *Built*, *Changed*, *Settled*.
- **Dates:** work dates. A range means the work spanned those days.
- **Rule since day one:** engine behavior is claimed only from the decompiled game or a field test.

<Shot label="Hero image for the timeline: the first chairlift line that drew in the game, next to the finished resort." />

## August

- **Aug 4 · Built** First chairlift line that draws in the game.
- **Aug 10 · Built** Ski Resort menu tab under Parks & Recreation, with the attraction score, the snow line and the first trail surfaces.
- **Aug 10 · Built** First building: an A-frame base lodge.
- **Aug 11 · Changed** The lodge became an upgrade of one resort entity, the Welcome Sign. One entity gives the resort its identity, and its size follows from what is built. Size tiers S, M and L were rejected.
- **Aug 11 · Built** Runs first show snow. The game's snow simulation carries the white, and decals carry the corduroy.
- **Aug 19 · Built** First full loop in the game: Welcome Sign, lodge, stations, lift, runs, power and economy all work together.

## October 1-9

- **Sep 30-Oct 1 · Changed** Run rating rewritten after play found green runs too hard to draw. The rating now follows the hardest unavoidable pitch, with bands at 6, 25, 40 and 60 %.
- **Oct 1 · Built** Ski Resort hub, the first in-game panel. The sign was hard to find, so the tools were combined into one panel.
- **Oct 4 · Built** Natural run, ski-area attraction score, ticket fees and a redesigned hub.
- **Oct 5 · Built** Ski days. In testing, a town lodge drew 162 guests a month and the real hill drew 2. Residents now choose the nearest venue of the leisure type they want, so the lodge claims a share of leisure trips.

## October 10-19

- **Oct 11 · Built** Five lodges, Ski School, patrol hut and patrol HQ, and the Welcome Sign.
- **Oct 11 · Built** Tram stations designed from a lift-engineering reference, with every platform reachable.
- **Oct 11-12 · Built** Grand Lodge: a U-plan around a pool court, with a plaza, a valet drive and vanilla trees and benches.
- **Oct 14 · Changed** Tickets are booked as public-transport fares, so ticket cash shows in the Economy panel.
- **Oct 14 · Built** Season passes, priced at about 65 % of lift revenue, bought once and not refundable.
- **Oct 15 · Built** Economy numbers approved: ticket 40 to 60, lodge spend 15, a lesson fee and pre-season pass sales. Lodges and Ski School carry upkeep and staff.
- **Oct 16 · Changed** Snow rules set: guns make snow up to 3.3 °C, and a run opens at 15 cm of snow.
- **Oct 16 · Built** One shared tile sheet for all eight buildings, with walls close to vanilla texel density inside hard triangle caps.
- **Oct 18 · Changed** Tram stations switched to plain glass, because lit fake interiors looked wrong at night. The stations got their own tile sheet.
- **Oct 18 · Built** Hub history: guests and finances per month.
- **Oct 19 · Changed** Hub redesign: a single net figure, a simpler Finances chart and a clearer History chart.
- **Oct 19 · Changed** Windows became plain glass with lit backing plates. Vanilla's fake room interiors read wrong in a ski building.
- **Oct 19 · Built** Tower planner: each tower's height follows the terrain along the line.
- **Oct 19 · Changed** Ski School becomes a top-level building.
- **Oct 19 · Built** All eight buildings and both tram stations in the game on their tile sheets.
- **Oct 19 · Settled** Vanilla sub-objects used wherever one exists, since they carry more detail for less effort.
- **Oct 20 · Changed** A near-full-year playthrough set the numbers for release: every run opens at 3 ft (91 cm), snow guns run October to March, ticket 80, lessons 30 %, season pass 2 tickets.
- **Oct 20 · Changed** The five lodges and both patrol buildings became standalone buildings, like the Ski School, instead of Welcome Sign upgrades.
- **Oct 20 · Built** One lift tool for all four lift types, numbered towers and a cat track grading tool.
