T200 snow report: layout options (mockup, sample numbers)

Each card sits beside the game's selected-info panel when a Welcome Sign is selected (the Ski Bus card's box). Option A's table is also drawn as the hub's SNOW fold (T201 #7). The dashed row is optional (needs one more climate scan, no save). "New snow, 7 days" is left out on purpose: one game day is one calendar month, so 7 days would be 7 months and equal the season total.
