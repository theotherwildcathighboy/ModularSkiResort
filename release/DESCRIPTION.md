# Modular Ski Resort — listing copy (draft)

*Paradox Mods long description. Keep claims matched to shipped features; update at upload.*

---

**Build a real ski resort in Cities: Skylines II — starting with the game's first working
chairlift.**

Draw a lift line up any mountain like a road: towers pick their own height to match the
terrain (8/12/16 m masts on concrete foundations), the haul rope sags and sways in the
wind, and chairs hang from the cable between a base and top station. Power flows up the
line from your grid. Everything connects, snaps, and validates like a native network — no
Anarchy required on sensible terrain.

**In this pack**
- Drawable fixed-grip chairlift network: auto-height towers, sagging cable, chairs
- Base and Top lift stations (4K textures, recolorable steel and safety padding)
- Base Lodge — the resort anchor (parks & recreation attraction)
- Groomed and snowmaking run surfaces for laying out trails
- A dedicated Ski Resort build tab under Parks & Recreation

**How to build (short version)**
Place your stations, draw the line between them — or draw the line first and snap the
stations onto its ends; both work. Towers, cables, and chairs handle themselves. Full
guide included on the mod page.

**Honest notes**
- Fixed-grip lifts refuse grades over 80% — that's aerial tram terrain (future pack)
- Push a lift somewhere absurd and you'll get absurd results, exactly like vanilla
  power lines in the same places
- Chairs don't move (yet) — that's the roadmap headline
- Best experienced with Tourism Overhaul (credits below)
- Lifts are power-line networks: they show in the electricity overlay, the base station acts
  as a transformer (wire a low-voltage line into its stub), and the line carries power up the
  mountain. Do not connect city high-voltage lines to a lift tower. Remove all lifts before
  uninstalling the mod.

**Companion code mod** (required, bundled): keeps chairs pinned to the cable, bridges
station power into the electricity grid, and carries the seasonal/snow-line groundwork.

**Credits**: inspired by the Modular Golf Course Pack; tourism integration designed
